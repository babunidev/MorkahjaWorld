# Plants and Materials

The plants, minerals, and crafted substances of Morkahja carry the elemental qualities of their origins and the work performed upon them. Some are known chiefly through surviving objects; their wider distribution remains uncharted.

## Whistle Reed

A rare reed can be cut into a whistle whose tone calls a spectral deer. Accounts describe that deer carrying a rider to a secluded grove. The plant's habitat has not been established. See [Whistle Reed](../Objects/Instruments_and_Resonance.md#whistle-reed).

## Titanvine and Evergrove Vines

Titanvine supplies the strong woven strands of the [Sturdy Net](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#sturdy-net). Resilient [Evergrove](../Places/Evergrove.md) vines form the [Verdani Whip](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#verdani-whip), with crystals set into its tip to sever ties of dark magic. These are distinct named materials; no shared species has been established.

## Woods of Craft

Ironwood forms the [Grimnir Boltcaster](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#grimnir-boltcaster). Wood of the Eldertree forms the [Banebow](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#banebow), whose string uses [Nagar](../Creatures/Nagar.md) boar tendons. Darkwood is worked into keys; moonwood provides the steps of the [Faewoven Ladder](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#faewoven-ladder). Blackened Fyrskar-wood forms the [Frostbane Glaive](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#frostbane-glaive)'s shaft. Their uses establish craft traditions without fixing the plants' taxonomy.

## Enchanted Metals and Crystals

Celestial metal forms the [Aetherglaive](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#aetherglaive)'s blade. Storm-forged steel and enchanted yew form the [Stormtamer](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#stormtamer) crossbow. Everfrost crystal is worked into the [Frostflame Key](../Objects/Keys_and_Seals.md#frostflame-key). The [Titanpike](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#titanpike) is described as forged from the heart of a fallen star. Sunstone, opal, quartz, and other gems also carry light, warmth, resonance, or powers of wayfinding in named objects.

## Nourishing Plants

Evergrove berries are deep purple and sweet. Enchanted roots nourish and help restore the wounded. Etherfruit provides both food and magical energy, particularly associated with [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) nourishment. Fyrskar herbs are used in [Emberheart Salve](../Objects/Food_and_Healing.md#emberheart-salve). Winter berries and frosty herbs flavor [Glacier Goulash](../Objects/Food_and_Healing.md#glacier-goulash); its other principal ingredient is arctic venison.

See [food and healing](../Objects/Food_and_Healing.md) and [weapons and tools](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md).

## Silks and Grasses

Frostwind spider silk is woven into the Frostweave Cloak. Silken grass forms the quieting weave of the Whispering Cowl. Verdant grasses and wildflowers are used in the Whisper Cloak. These materials are known through their uses; their cultivation and wider ecology remain open.
