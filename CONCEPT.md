# MorkahjaWorld — Concept

**Creative:** Babuni

## The world

Morkahja is my original fantasy world: young and primal, full of elemental forces, magic, exploration, forgotten histories, and living relationships between the natural and the supernatural.

Fire, Water, Earth, and Air shape the world, while Aether connects and transforms those forces. The Elemental Pantheon and the four peoples—Rokur, Verdani, Aevis, and Grimnir—provide important foundations. Ethera, a living creation of the pantheon, is part of Morkahja's own lore; her presence is not merely a reference to another repository.

These are high-level orientation points drawn from the existing [World Identity](Overview/World_Identity.md) and [World Overview](Overview/World_Overview.md). The individual lore documents, not this project description, hold the details and their unresolved questions.

## Purpose of the repository

Keeping the world material in one navigable collection makes its places, cultures, creatures, events, and ideas easier to explore and preserve. The archive is written in Markdown, connected by subject indexes and cross-references.

This is **world-building documentation**, not evidence of a finished game, a software architecture, or a fixed plan to make one.

## Creative boundaries

Some entries are established descriptions; others preserve questions, alternatives, and mysteries. An unanswered question should not be silently resolved by an editor or AI assistant.

Other Babuni projects may share names or history with MorkahjaWorld, but their files and designs are not automatically adopted as part of this archive. Any later consolidation should document its source and my creative decision.
