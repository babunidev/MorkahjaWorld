# Clothing and Protection

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Frostfire Mantle

A rugged, azure cloak imbued with magic. It radiates a comfortable warmth, protecting its wearer from the biting cold, and glows faintly in the darkness.

## Avalanche Aegis

A sturdy, circular shield shimmering with ethereal blue energy. Its surface gleams with ancient symbols, promising protection. Its protective enchantment is associated with the dangers of the mountain slopes.

## Forest Cloak

A deep-green cloak, woven with intricate patterns of silver thread, which shimmers under moonlight. Features a clasp made from a polished, wolf-shaped piece of obsidian.

## Frostweave Cloak

This resplendent cloak, woven from the silken threads of Frostwind spiders, glistens with an icy sheen, providing perfect camouflage against the snowy backdrop of the mountains.

## Whisper Cloak

A fluid cloak woven from verdant grass and interspersed with wildflowers. It seamlessly blends with the meadow, radiating a calming aura to pacify wild creatures.

## Whispering Cowl

A supple, earth-toned hood, intricately woven from the noise-dampening threads of silken-grass. Its quieting properties offer protection against the call of a [Yowler](../Creatures/Wolves_and_Liani.md#yowler).

## Embrace Cloak

Woven with lichen-green threads and lined with the thick fur of tundra fauna, this resilient garment provides warmth and camouflage amidst craggy landscape.

## Frostcloak

A shimmering, snow-white cloak made of ethereal fabric. Its ghostly aura disorients the witness, while imbuing the wearer with a soothing, calming presence.

## Shattershield Lantern

Encased in a silver filigree, this lantern emits a pulsing light that disorients and weakens foes, shattering their focus and leaving them vulnerable.

## Whispercloak Dagger

This slender blade is sheathed in shadow, its hilt wrapped in sound-dampening cloth, ensuring that each strike is silent as a whisper.

## Chillshard Gauntlet

Forged from enchanted ice and reinforced steel, this gauntlet emits a frigid aura, capable of extinguishing any fiery magic and neutralizing chaotic energy.

## Galebreaker Shield

This shield, forged from storm-resistant alloys and enchanted with calming runes, repels the tempestuous onslaught, offering protection against the harrowing forces unleashed.

## Celestial Cloak

This luminous cloak, woven from starlight threads, offers protection and strength, its celestial energy designed to bolster magic and shield the spirit.

## Emberward Cloak

This cloak, woven from fire-resistant fibers and enchanted for protection, shields its wearer from both magical and physical harm, ideal for those battling in perilous environments.

## Windward Bulwark

A pendant encased in swirling silver, its core a shard of crystallized air. When invoked, it forms a barrier of swirling winds, deflecting harm and freeing the ensnared.

## Whisperstep Boots

Boots crafted from supple leather and enchanted with a subtle magic that muffles footsteps. Perfect for traversing treacherous terrain and evading the gaze of lurking horrors.

## Shadowstep Boots

Crafted from supple leather and enchanted with a subtle magic that cloaks the wearer in shadows, these boots allow for silent movement and nimble evasion.
