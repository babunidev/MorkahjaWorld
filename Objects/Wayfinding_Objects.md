# Wayfinding Objects

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Secret Map

A weathered parchment depicting the landscape somewhere in [Ethera's Vale](../Places/Etheras_Vale.md), marked with cryptic symbols hinting at hidden treasures and secrets to whomever watches closely. An older name, Hidden Secrets Map, associates this parchment with the [Enchanted Glades](../Places/Vale_Landmarks.md#enchanted-glades).

## Enchanted Guide

A meticulously detailed map revealing the hidden wonders and elusive treasures nestled within the mystical surroundings, beckoning adventurers to embark on enchanting journeys.

## Explorer's Chart

An illustrated chart of Ethera's Vale, recording landscapes and hints of concealed wonders. An older name, Meadowland Explorer Chart, associates it with the [Lovely Meadows](../Places/Vale_Landmarks.md#lovely-meadows).

## Mystic Map

A weathered parchment displaying the enigmatic terrain of Ethera's Vale, marked with cryptic symbols and clues that hint at concealed riches awaiting intrepid explorers.

## Seraphic Map

An aged parchment revealing the celestial landscapes of Ethera's Vale, adorned with celestial motifs and inscrutable hints leading to hidden treasures. The older Seraphic Planes Map spelling refers to the [Seraphic Plains](../Places/Vale_Landmarks.md#seraphic-plains); it does not establish a separate realm.

## Meadow Star

A pendant inscribed with an intricate map of Ethera's Vale. Aligning the pendant with the constellations at certain times reveals the path to a hidden treasure trove.

## Fairyseer Compass

A delicate, translucent orb mounted on a finely carved wooden base. Embedded within the orb are iridescent fairy wings, creating a mesmerizing, ever-shifting kaleidoscope of colors that points to hidden treasures.

## Glademyst Geode

A hollow, hand-sized stone of shimmering lavender hues. The inside is filled with crystalline points that glow with a soft, ethereal light, pulsating brighter as it nears hidden treasures.

## Echosight Amulet

A delicate silver amulet encasing a luminous opal. It vibrates gently, guiding its bearer towards concealed treasures by amplifying echoes. Its echo-amplifying quality is particularly associated with cliffside searches.

## Peakfinder Prism

This hand-held device is a work of magical artistry. A crystalline prism, surrounded by intricate silver filigree, it hums with a soft, icy glow, guiding adventurers to hidden treasures. It is associated with the [Mountain Pass](../Places/Frostwind_Landmarks.md#mountain-pass) in older descriptions.

## Ohgar Sunstone

This luminescent orange crystal, shaped like a drop of the sun, seems to pulse with an inner heat. When held aloft, it projects a beam that uncovers hidden passages, revealing untold treasures.

## Whispergem

A captivating, azure gem held in a silver casing, it pulsates gently in the hand. When close to the treasure, it hums harmoniously, guiding the bearer towards the bounty.

## Beacon Amulet

A teardrop-shaped pendant made of shimmering quartz, held by a rugged brass casing. It glows brighter as a treasure draws nearer.

## Divining Locket

A delicate silver locket, adorned with a glowing ruby centerpiece that pulses in time with the heartbeat of concealed treasure.

## Frostfire Compass

An intricate compass of sapphire and silver, glowing softly with a spectral blue flame. It pulsates rhythmically, growing stronger when facing hidden treasures.

## Lumina Compass

An ornate compass with a radiant crystal center, it pulsates softly, guiding seekers to hidden magical treasures.

## Guiding Star Pendant

This pendant features a miniature, glowing orb that oscillates between shades of blue and white, guiding its bearer unerringly to hidden magic items.

## Beacon of Hope

This amulet, encrusted with a luminescent crystal, pulses with a warm light, cutting through the gloom to reveal hidden magic items.

## Sunseeker Lantern

An ornate lantern with a ghostly flame that flickers in response to hidden magic, guiding its bearer through treacherous expanse towards concealed enchantments.

## Echoes Compass

A compass with a spectral needle that points towards magical relics, resonating with the whispers of the fallen.

## Loreseeker's Lantern

This lantern, with a flame that burns in brilliant blues and greens, reveals hidden inscriptions and magical paths, guiding to lost knowledge and items.

## Echoing Compass

A delicate, silver compass, its needle spinning not to magnetic north but to guide the bearer through mystical enchantments, pulsing with a soft, otherworldly glow.
