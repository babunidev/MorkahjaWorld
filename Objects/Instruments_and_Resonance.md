# Instruments and Resonance

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Whistle Reed

A rare plant when cut and fashioned into a whistle, its ethereal tone calls forth a ghostly deer. Riding this spectral creature allows the rider to reach a secluded grove brimming with magical artifacts.

## Whistle of Harmony

This whistle emits an ethereal melody that can resonate with hidden energy ley lines, prompting the ground to shift and reveal hidden paths or treasure chests.

## Dreamy Lyre

A magical instrument that, when strummed with a melody of ancient lore, opens a gateway to a hidden valley where time stands still.

## Whisperwind Flute

An enchanting musical instrument crafted from sun-bleached bone.

## Mesmerizing Windchime

A peculiar wind chime, crafted from the bones of a forgotten species. Its haunting melodies, played by the winds, unlock a hidden trove within [Ethera's Vale](../Places/Etheras_Vale.md).

## Echohorn

An antique horn that echoes through the surroundings. Its profound notes vibrate the stones.

## Harmony Lyre

An enchanting, weathered wooden lyre, its strings glistening under sunlight. Intricate carvings of [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) symbols decorate its surface, promising the power to mend hearts and restore harmony.

## Cry of Valor

A brass horn, etched with symbols of strength and unity. It glows warmly when held, and its resonant call can inspire courage in any heart.

## Zephyrstring Lyre

A lyre with strings spun from the essence of the elements, capable of summoning storms or calming breezes with its hauntingly beautiful melodies.

## Melody of Stonara

A finely crafted flute made from dark, petrified wood, adorned with runes that pulse with earthen magic. Playing it invokes soothing melodies that resonate with the essence of the earth.

## Nereval's Cascades

A flute, carved from azure crystal and adorned with vine-like engravings, its notes resonate with the clarity of flowing water, captivating the listener with sounds that echo the serene beauty of nature.
