# Chests and Vaults

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Aevis Chest

[Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) chests combine fine ornament with intricate locking mechanisms. One long-abandoned chest in [Eldertree Forest](../Places/Eldertree_Forest.md) has become enclosed by roots, moss, and flowers. Its proximity to [Celesterra](../Places/Celesterra.md) is reported in old accounts, but the precise relationship between the forest and the subterranean city remains unresolved.

## Aevis Lockbox

A lightweight, opalescent metal lockbox bears wind-like scrollwork, feather engravings, and a gilded crest. Accounts call it a royal lockbox, but the family or institution represented by the crest has not been established. It is associated with the forest approaches to Celesterra.

## Grimnir Trunk

Stone and worked metal form a heavy trunk decorated with geometric patterns and runes. A preserved example rests in an icy Frostwind cave near [Deepstone Hollow](../Places/Deepstone_Hollow.md). Its complex lock and durable construction express [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) craft and care for what must endure.

## Verdani Casket

A darkwood casket is carved with flowing plants and animals. Ferns and flowers conceal it within young woodland associated with Eldertree Forest and the approaches to [Evergrove](../Places/Evergrove.md). Its matching key is also carved from darkwood. The exact border described by this account is unsettled.

## Green-Bronze Chest

A greenish bronze chest among the wildflowers of the [Seraphic Plains](../Places/Vale_Landmarks.md#seraphic-plains) bears the workmanship of an unidentified earlier civilization. Its elaborate lock uses interlocking gears and levers. The origin of both chest and makers remains unknown.

## Wooden Treasure Chests

Common chests may be built from polished darkwood with little ornament beyond a substantial brass lock. Their construction preserves everyday woodworking and locksmithing traditions alongside the world's more elaborate enchanted containers.

## Carved Meadow Chest

A light wooden chest found in a meadow carries carvings of old stories and a brass lock. The maker, owners, and events represented by its images remain unidentified.

## Everwinter Chest

An old frostbite-timber chest in Everwinter bears glowing blue runes and floral carvings. Iron corners support it, while an enchantment radiates warmth despite the surrounding frost. Its makers and contents remain unknown.

## Eldritch Stronghold

Despite its name, the Eldritch Stronghold is a massive cube-like trunk of darksteel and old ironwood, resting among the roots of a dead tree in [Keldor](../Places/Keldor.md). Rusted locks and overlapping edges secure its moss-covered lid. It is not an established fortress or settlement.

## Ironclad Reliquary

An iron-banded darkwood trunk lies beneath Keldor's canopy. Engravings, verdigris, and moss cover its lid and fittings. A dormant magical presence is reported within it, but its contents and makers remain unknown.
