# Food and Healing

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Healing Elixir

A clear, soothing elixir contained in a simple glass vial. It possesses the ability to mend wounds and restore vitality when consumed.

## Soothing Wrap

This soft and gentle wrap is designed to aid in the healing of wounds. It provides comfort and support during the recovery process.

## Verdani Sourdough

A rustic and flavorful bread crafted by the [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md), known for its delightful blend of earthy and tangy flavors, a culinary treat in the mystical world.

## Crystal Clear Elixir

A crystal-clear elixir contained within a magical vessel, quenching thirst with revitalizing and purifying properties in the mystical world.

## Emberheart Salve

A healing balm housed in a compact jar. Formulated from Fyrskar herbs and imbued with soothing magic, it's known to heal burns and rejuvenate the fatigued.

## Wolfsbane Elixir

A gleaming glass vial containing a luminescent blue potion. Its swirling azure depths hint at potent magical properties and promise safety in the perilous wilderness.

## Glacier Goulash

Glacier Goulash is a hearty meal served in a large, frosted bowl. It shimmers with magical, icy light and radiates delicious aromas of winter berries, frosty herbs, and arctic venison.

## Celestial Salve

A luminescent blue jar, its lid crowned with an antler motif. Within, a radiant gel sparkles like the night sky, emanating a soothing, cold, and enchanted aura.

## Nourishroot Pouch

This small pouch contains enchanted roots that, when consumed, provide sustenance and healing properties, ideal for mending injuries and revitalizing the weary.

## Etherfruit Satchel

This satchel contains ethereal fruits that nourish with both sustenance and magical energy, ideal for replenishing strength and focus. The fruit is especially associated with the nourishment of [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md).

## Hearthguard Stew

A nourishing stew prepared from local root vegetables, wild herbs, and tender hunted meat.

## Healing Dewdrop Pendant

Within a crystal vial suspended by a chain of woven silver, a luminescent azure essence ebbs, its gentle light dancing like morning sunlight on the water's surface.

## Rokur Salve

Contained within a small, earthen jar, this vibrant, red ointment pulses with a warm, healing glow, promising to mend wounds and rejuvenate the weary spirit of any who apply it.

## Evergrove Berries

A small, simple pouch, bursting with the deep purple hues of berries. Its contents promise the sweetness of nature's bounty.
