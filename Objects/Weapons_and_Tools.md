# Weapons and Tools

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Windwhisper Bow

A sleek and elegant [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) longbow that grants its user the ability to curve their arrows' trajectory.

## Earthshaker Pickaxe

A versatile and durable [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) pickaxe that can be used as both a tool and a weapon.

## Stonecrusher Mace

A heavy and sturdy Grimnir mace, with a head carved from solid rock.

## Gilded Flamehammer

A mighty [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) hammer with a head that ignites in flames when swung.

## Pyroclast Javelin

A long, lightweight Rokur spear that leaves a trail of fire in its wake.

## Moonshadow Daggers

[Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) twin daggers imbued with the essence of darkest water.

## Tidecaller Staff

A magical Verdani staff topped with a crystal orb that channels the power of water.

## Aetherglaive

A remarkable Aevis polearm featuring a blade forged from celestial metal. It resonates with ethereal energy, allowing it to cleave through the air and disrupt magical energies with unparalleled precision.

## Climbing Gear

A set of essential equipment for climbing, including sturdy ropes, carabiners, harnesses, and grip-enhancing gloves, ensuring safety and efficiency during ascents.

## Verdani Whip

A flexible whip, woven from resilient [Evergrove](../Places/Evergrove.md) vines, its tip embedded with shimmering crystals capable of severing dark magic ties.

## Grimnir Runeblade

A finely crafted blade, etched with powerful Grimnir runes, its edge glowing with a light that can slice through magic. Older accounts describe the blade cutting through shadowy fear.

## Glacial Halberd

An enchanted halberd, crafted by the skilled Rokur. The blade radiates frost magic, countering fire and capable of freezing even the most fiery of foes.

## Grimnir Boltcaster

A hefty crossbow made of sturdy ironwood, with intricate carvings of boar and huntsman along its stock. The quarrel groove glows faintly, ready to launch enchanted, barbed bolts with deadly precision.

## Baneglaive

An imposing weapon, it features a lengthy ash wood haft capped with a razor-sharp blade, shaped to mirror the ferocity of a charging boar.

## Banebow

Crafted from the resilient wood of the Eldertree, this elegant longbow showcases an intricate design. Its string, woven from [Nagar Boar](../Creatures/Nagar.md#nagar-boar) tendons, is remarkably strong, while the tips are inlaid with shimmering emeralds that capture and intensify the wielder's aim.

## Sturdy Net

A robust, wide-spanning net made from interwoven strands of titanvine.

## Rokur Crossbow

This masterfully crafted weapon bears the robustness of heavy oak and the intricacy of finely woven silver wire. Its runes pulsate with protective enchantments.

## Titanpike

Forged from the heart of a fallen star, this elongated, metallic pike gleams under the sun.

## Hornbane

A compact, yet robust crossbow with shimmering steel limbs and a mahogany stock. Its quiver is filled with serrated bolts, designed to penetrate the thickest of hides.

## Stormtamer

Crafted from storm-forged steel and enchanted yew, this crossbow boasts a formidable power. Some accounts describe bolts tipped with a tranquilizing elixir, used to halt a [Thunderhoof](../Creatures/Nagar.md#thunderhoof).

## Crescent Blade

A gracefully curved sword, its hilt wrapped in white leather. The shimmering blue blade resonates with a soft, ethereal glow, promising swift victory.

## Verdant Staff

An elegant staff intricately carved from ancient Liani hardwood. Its gnarled surface is entwined with vibrant moss and luminescent fungi, emitting a soft, soothing glow.

## Serpentine Bow

Crafted from ancient wood and silver, this bow's slender form shimmers with an ethereal sheen. Its arrows, when notched, emit a faint emerald glow.

## Stormweaver Net

A large, intricately woven net shimmering with energy, designed to safely capture and contain. It sparkles with conductive threads absorbing power. Older descriptions connect its conductive weave with absorbing the [Thunderfly Matriarch](../Creatures/Karrhu_and_Winged_Creatures.md#thunderfly-matriarch)’s electrical power.

## Duskwhisper Crossbow

A sleek, ebony crossbow, inlaid with moonstone. Its design features sound-dampening components for silent shots, ideal for neutralizing alarmed creatures.

## Frostbite Hook

A beautifully crafted hook made from glacial metal, its handle wound with fur for grip. When thrown, the hook glows, creating ice handholds.

## Frostbane Glaive

A magnificent weapon pulsating with fiery energy. The shaft is made from blackened Fyrskar-wood, while its crescent blade glows with a molten ember hue, capable of melting through the coldest of hearts.

## Frostweaver Hook

A sturdy, silver-tinted grappling hook with a long, supple leather rope. Its handle is etched with ancient Frostweaver glyphs, and it radiates a subtle chill.

## Glacier Grapnel

An intricately carved, silver-blue hook attached to a woven, shimmering rope. Radiating a faint cold aura, it clings to ice with supernatural grip, enabling ascent on slick surfaces.

## Faewoven Ladder

A silvery, luminescent rope ladder with steps made of sturdy moonwood. Enchanted leaves flutter around it, ensuring safe ascent. Its glow beckons climbers to hidden heights.

## Verdant Heart Lance

A sleek spear, its head forged from pure silver, adorned with a vial of sacred essence that neutralizes vile magic.

## Gleam Net

Woven from strands of moonbeam-infused silk, this ethereal net disrupts magical energies, ensnaring and draining a creature's unnatural vigor.

## Shadowstalker Bow

Crafted from the darkest yew and strung with a silken thread, this bow allows for silent, precise shots, perfect for hunting.

## Seeker's Grapnel

This lightweight, yet sturdy, grappling hook is designed for precision, effortlessly securing to even the most precarious holds.

## Frostveil Blade

This ethereal sword, shimmering with a frosty aura, is designed to counteract fire, its icy touch capable of quelling even the fiercest magic.

## Solstice Flareblade

Forged from rare, sun-kissed metal, this radiant blade emits a comforting warmth, countering the harsh chill of cold environments.

## Dawnbreaker Lance

Forged in light and inscribed with runes of banishment, this lance's radiant tip pierces through darkness, a beacon of hope.

## Ember Shard Anvil

A small, obsidian anvil, imbued with a core of eternal flame, allowing it to manipulate and forge materials infused with elemental magic.
