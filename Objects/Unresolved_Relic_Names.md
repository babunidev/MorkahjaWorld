# Unresolved Relic Names

These names occur in existing lore notes but lack settled descriptions. Their associations are provisional and do not establish an object's appearance, power, ownership, or exact location.

## Northern Foods

Hearthroot Feast is associated with the hospitality of [Frostbite Fergus](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#frostbite-fergus). Frostberry Banquet is associated with the guardian of [Everwinter Cave](../Places/Everwinter_Cave.md). Their ingredients and relationship to [Glacier Goulash](Food_and_Healing.md#glacier-goulash) remain undefined.

## Frostweaver Objects

Frostweaver Resonance Compass is associated with Everwinter Cave. Sigil Lens of [Nekalos](../World_History/Frostweaver_Lore.md#nekalos) is associated with interpreting the [Seal of Nekalos](Frostweaver_Relics.md#seal-of-nekalos). [Atranor](../Places/Atranor.md)'s Beacon is associated with Kaldric, but its nature and relationship to the four sacred Atranor treasures remain unknown.

Everkey of Frostweaver's Memory and Frostweaver's Grappling Hook are older descriptive names associated with the [Frostforged Vault](../Places/Frostforged_Vault.md) and [Skyreach Peak](../Places/Skyreach_Peak.md). Their exact identity relative to [Frostweaver's Everkey](Keys_and_Seals.md#frostweavers-everkey) and [Frostweaver Hook](Weapons_and_Tools.md#frostweaver-hook) remains to be reconciled.

## Objects of Hope and Performance

Songseed Flute, Emberheart War Drum, Moonchime Harp, and Stonefire Standard are associated respectively with a raided [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) village, a disheartened [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) warrior, [Eternal Twilight Grove](../Places/Eternal_Twilight_Grove.md), and [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) resistance. Their construction and specific histories remain open.

## Other Everwinter Names

Sunshard Spear is associated with the [Icebound Nymph Queen](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#icebound-nymph-queen). Crystalheart Elixir is associated with the [Shattered Seer](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#the-shattered-seer). Banner of Unyielding Dawn is associated with weary mountain travelers. These associations alone do not establish a reliable magical effect.
