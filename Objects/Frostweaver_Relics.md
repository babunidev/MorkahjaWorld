## Seal of Nekalos

The Knowledge Seal of Nekalos is an inscription table of icy-blue crystalline stone within the Frostweaver palace ruins. Its glyphs communicate through visions and sensations as well as words. It preserves knowledge of the lost civilization without revealing the full identity of [Nekalos](../World_History/Frostweaver_Lore.md#nekalos).
