# Sacred Relics

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Atranor Elixir

This elixir, with its vibrant colors and swirling patterns, exudes an aura of potency. It's a brew of great power, promising remarkable effects when consumed.

## Atranor Scroll

A scroll inscribed with powerful runes and arcane knowledge. It is one of the four sacred treasures associated with [Atranor](../Places/Atranor.md).

## Atranor Stone

An enigmatic gemstone radiating immense magical potential. It is one of the four sacred treasures associated with Atranor.

## Atranor Dragontooth

A formidable dragon's tooth, imbued with ancient power, serving as a symbol of strength and ferocity. It exudes an aura of untamed might.

## Offerings in Atranor

The four sacred treasures are beloved by the Pantheon. A soul carrying one at death may offer it during judgement and be granted another chance at life. No fixed condition guarantees that the gods accept an offering. Atranor’s Beacon and Atranor’s Lament are separate names whose relationship to these four remains unknown.
