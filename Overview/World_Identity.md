# World Identity of Morkahja

Morkahja is a young, primal world rich in magic, elemental forces, rare treasures, ancient mysteries, and exploration. It is wondrous and serious, with a slightly dark atmosphere shaped by forgotten history, supernatural phenomena, and the later corruption of magic.

The world is built from relationships between the four material elements: Fire, Water, Earth, and Air. Aether acts as quintessence, connecting those elements through magic, inspiration, and higher transformation. Natural and supernatural forces are not separate systems in Morkahja; they overlap in creatures, places, civilizations, and encounters.
