# Nereval's Sanctuary

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Location:** [Ethera's Vale](Etheras_Vale.md)  
**Classification:** [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) sacred site, healing sanctuary, sealed temple  
**Associated deity:** Nereval, God of Water  
**Elemental reading:** Water, Growth, Spirit, healing, preservation

Nereval's Sanctuary is a hidden Verdani holy site concealed within the central meadows of [Ethera's Vale](Etheras_Vale.md). Its entrance is marked by a simple ancient stone door, protected by a magical seal that cannot be opened through force or ordinary lockwork.

The seal responds to an act of healing. The act may involve a wounded creature, a damaged water source, a sick plant, or a person in need. Verdani tradition teaches that the sanctuary does not measure the scale of the healing, but the sincerity and understanding behind it.

## The Sanctuary Grounds

The surrounding meadows contain clear streams, ancient natural formations, moss-covered stones, and quiet pools where elemental currents rise close to the surface. Verdani guardians care for the area without reshaping it into a formal settlement.

The sanctuary itself is used for recovery, pilgrimage, water rites, and the preservation of memory. It is not a temple of conquest or public authority. Its purpose is to restore what can be restored and to recognize what must be allowed to change.

## Open Possibilities

- The seal may respond to healing performed through a skill, item, or act of personal sacrifice.
- The sanctuary may preserve memories from the earliest agreement between the Pantheon and the mortal races.
- A damaged stream near the sanctuary may be connected to the elemental currents beneath [Aetherion](Aetherion.md).
- The Verdani guardians may refuse entry to anyone who treats healing as a transaction rather than a responsibility.

## The Stone Door

The sanctuary's ancient stone doorway is inscribed in a forgotten language and sealed with a soft light. The air beyond carries the scent of rain on fertile earth. Healing and compassion have a place in its rites of admission.
