# Keldor

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Cursed forest region  
**Known association:** [Liani Shadowwolf](../Creatures/Wolves_and_Liani.md#liani-shadowwolf)  
**Major anomaly:** [Blackwood Abyss](Blackwood_Abyss.md)  
**Related world event:** [The Urcogor Ravage](../World_Events/Urcogor_Ravage.md)  
**Elemental reading:** Darkness, Void, corrupted Nature, damaged Spirit

Keldor is a cursed forest region enclosed by distant mountain ranges and blanketed in permanent fog. Its wilderness is hostile, mutable, and difficult to map. Trees grow in unnatural directions, wildlife behaves erratically, and travelers often report memory loss, distorted time, and the sensation of being watched.

Keldor is associated with Liani Shadowwolf, a rare mystical wolf embodying shadow and forgotten wisdom. Liani is said to test worthiness before revealing ancient truths, though whether she protects travelers from Keldor or protects Keldor from travelers remains uncertain.

The region's deepest known anomaly is the [Blackwood Abyss](Blackwood_Abyss.md), a forbidden subterranean labyrinth reached through the elusive Blackwood Portal. Local accounts describe the abyss as both place and presence, possibly an ancient buried organism or containment site beneath the forest.

Keldor's curse appears to operate through more than physical corruption. In its deeper regions, natural mutation gives way to psychological intrusion: voices, false memories, impossible structures glimpsed through fog, and the feeling that something beneath the earth is aware.

Some scholars also connect Keldor to the Urcogor Ravage. If the Ravage is awakened by ancient presences seeping from forgotten places, Keldor is one of the most likely regions where such a force could gather.

## Known Features

- Permanent fog and unreliable distance perception.
- Warped trees and mutated forest growth.
- Erratic or altered wildlife.
- Memory disruption and distorted time.
- The hidden Blackwood Portal.
- The Blackwood Abyss beneath the forest.
- Border sites where [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) healers have treated survivors.

## Open Possibilities

- Keldor may preserve ancient truths hidden beneath shadow and memory.
- Liani Shadowwolf tests worthiness before revealing forgotten wisdom.
- The curse of Keldor may connect to corrupted magic, Umbrael's hidden realms, Void-touched space, or an older tragedy.
- The Blackwood Abyss may be the true source of the region's curse.
- Maps of Keldor may become unreliable because the forest's geography is unstable near the Blackwood Portal.
- Dormant Urco, Gobbls, Ogrus, or hybrid bloodlines may be hidden beneath Keldor's roots.
