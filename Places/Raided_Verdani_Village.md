# Raided Verdani Village

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Damaged settlement  
**Associated people:** [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md)

This [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) village was attacked by Ogrus and Gobbls, possibly as a small local echo of the [Urcogor Ravage](../World_Events/Urcogor_Ravage.md). Survivors need hope, inspiration, and restoration after the raid.

## Relic Traditions

- Songseed Flute - item tied to inspiring survivors and restoring hope.

## The Surviving Tree

The village is an [Evergrove](Evergrove.md) community devastated by an Ogru and Gobbl raid. Charred structures stand around an unharmed elder tree, which remains important to the survivors' hope. Its own name and the date of the raid are unrecorded.
