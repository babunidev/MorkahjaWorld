# Frostwind Mountains

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Frozen mountain range  
**Known location:** Contains [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md)

The Frostwind Mountains are a harsh frozen range defined by constant cold, treacherous passes, and ancient secrets beneath snow and ice. Their most infamous region is [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md), a permafrost basin filled with Frostweaver ruins, dangerous creatures, and hidden treasure.

## Known Sites

- [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md)
- [Skyreach Peak](Skyreach_Peak.md)
- [Frostfall Cascade](Frostfall_Cascade.md)

## Rockslides

Rockfalls can abruptly close routes through the Frostwind Mountains. Falling boulders carry snow and dust down the slopes, leaving altered paths and unstable debris. Familiar routes cannot always be trusted after such an event.
