# Fyrskar Mountains

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Northern volcanic glacier mountain system  
**Associated people:** [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md)  
**Known features:** Volcanic ridges, glaciers, ancient calderas, geothermal ravines, mountain citadels, hearth networks

The Fyrskar Mountains are the homeland of the [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md), an immense northern mountain system where volcanic ridges rise through glaciers and ancient calderas sleep beneath kilometers of ice.

To outsiders, Fyrskar appears impossible. Snowstorms consume valleys for weeks. Jagged peaks split cloud layers. Frozen cliffs descend into geothermal ravines where rivers of heated water carve through ice older than civilization itself.

To the Rokur, the landscape is not hostile. It is necessary.

The north acts as a living counterbalance to Rokur elemental nature. Cold air slows thermal escalation, glacial environments regulate internal flame cycles, and mountain interiors allow entire cities to be designed around airflow, thermal release, and environmental balance.

Rokur settlements rarely stand upon mountains. They exist inside them: cliff walls, volcanic chambers, mountain interiors, stone arteries beneath peaks, and hearth chambers that burn endlessly at settlement centers.

## Known Features

- Volcanic glacier systems.
- Ancient calderas beneath ice.
- Geothermal ravines.
- Hearth chambers and hearth networks.
- Cliff-wall cities and mountain interiors.
- Bridges crossing impossible distances.

## Open Possibilities

- The Fyrskar Mountains shaped Rokur identity after the Flamebreakings.
- Environmental cold suppresses excessive heat accumulation and helps stabilize Heartfires.
- Thermal surges, avalanches, blizzards, and enclosed heat accumulation threaten settlements.
- Rokur are famous for traversing Fyrskar through immense leaps between cliffs, ravines, and peaks.

## Rocky Tundra

Brown crags rise from yellow tundra plains where moss, lichen, and scattered shrubs survive biting winds. The region is associated with the western or northern approaches to Fyrskar, but its border remains unresolved.
