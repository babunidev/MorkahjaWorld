# Everwinter Valley

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Permafrost valley  
**Region:** [Frostwind Mountains](Frostwind_Mountains.md)  
**Known features:** Constant snow and ice, Frostweaver ruins, dangerous wildlife, hidden treasures

Everwinter Valley is a frozen permafrost valley in the [Frostwind Mountains](Frostwind_Mountains.md). Snow and ice dominate the landscape, preserving ancient Frostweaver ruins beneath layers of frost and silence.

The valley is dangerous, but not empty. Travelers speak of sacred spirits, predatory nymphs, old ghosts, hungry trolls, intelligent primal wolves, lost scholars, hidden vaults, and relics of great value. Wealth and power await those who survive its cold.

## Frostweaver Lore

The valley contains ruins and relics tied to the Frostweavers, including [Nekalos](../World_History/Frostweaver_Lore.md#nekalos), the Nekalos Mechanism, the [Seal of Nekalos](../Objects/Frostweaver_Relics.md#seal-of-nekalos), and spectral figures such as [Kaldric of Nekalos](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#kaldric-of-nekalos) and [Lyria of Nekalos](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#lyria-of-nekalos).

## Local Conditions

- Fresh food is valuable at the southern trading post.
- Frostweaver relics may preserve ancient knowledge and forgotten practices.
- Sacred spirits and predatory spirits coexist in the frozen landscape.
- The Nekalos Mechanism is remembered by some ghosts as a mistake.
