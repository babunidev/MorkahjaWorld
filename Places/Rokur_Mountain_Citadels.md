# Rokur Mountain Citadels

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Mountain settlements  
**Associated people:** [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md)  
**Region:** [Fyrskar Mountains](Fyrskar_Mountains.md)  
**Known features:** Citadels, hearth-lit halls, hearth sanctums, ritual firecraft sites, geothermal chambers

The [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) dwell in mountain citadels and hearth-lit halls, places shaped around endurance, unity, and ritual firecraft. These settlements are both defensive strongholds and communal sanctuaries, built to contain and honor living flame.

Many Rokur citadels do not stand on mountains, but within them. Cities are carved into cliff walls, volcanic chambers, and mountain interiors where geothermal heat can be controlled and distributed through immense hearth networks.

The citadels express Rokur ideals of shared strength, discipline, and transformation through fire.

## Open Possibilities

- Hearth-lit halls may serve as communal, religious, and political centers.
- Rokur citadels could maintain forge relations or rivalries with [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) Skyland crafters.
- Ritual firecraft rites may be tied to Fyraan, Solriel, and Khaotys.
- Every citadel must manage airflow, thermal release, and environmental balance to prevent dangerous heat accumulation.
