# Karrhu

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Flytrap Grotto

A damp cavern in Karrhu reeks of decay and hums with flies. A bristled fly the size of a small horse is said to guard its interior. The creature has red eyes, damaged wings, and a proboscis reputed to drain life; stories call the grotto cursed.

## Quicksilver Quagmire

A nearly still circular pool lies among knotted swamp trees. At night its surface shines with silver light and mirrors the stars. Those who study it speak of insight into hidden capacities, but the nature and reliability of its visions are unknown.

## Aradia's Perch

A dangerous site within Karrhu is associated with the Aradia dragonfly. Silver markings catch sunlight over murky water. The creature's sting is strongly venomous; the perch's beauty has made it no less feared.
