# Aetherion Landmarks

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Hidden Market

A market reached through a secret tunnel brings unusual merchants and travelers together over enchanted gems, ornate weapons, old books, spices, and food. It is associated with [Aetherion](Aetherion.md)'s wider trade network, although its exact entrance is unplaced. Tales of its extraordinary clientele should not be read as a census of Morkahja's peoples.

## Market of Aetherion

Colored awnings shelter spices, fruit, woven cloth, and handmade goods. Bakers and street cooks work beside merchants and artisans. Bartering, laughter, and everyday social life make the market a civic gathering place as well as a center of exchange.

## Archive of Echoes

This library holds books imbued with traces of their creators. The voices of deceased authors whisper through its high aisles, allowing history to be heard as well as read. Accounts of the city's founding survive among the enchanted volumes.

## Aetherion Forge

[Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) smiths work in a sacred forge protected by glowing runes. Fire, cooling water, and elemental spirits participate in the making of enchanted objects. Hammering and steam accompany a craft understood as both material work and ritual.

## Garden of Elements

Flowered paths and clear streams pass among tall trees and stones glowing with inner light. The garden expresses elemental coexistence within Aetherion. Accounts also describe disturbances beneath its calm, showing that its harmony can require care and restoration.
