## The Grimnir Vault

A stone-and-metal vault lies within a cave in northern Deepstone Hollow, near [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md). Its door fits closely into the rock face, concealed by moss, lichen, and ice. An intricately shaped keystone belongs to its locking mechanism. The full extent and history of Deepstone Hollow remain unknown.
