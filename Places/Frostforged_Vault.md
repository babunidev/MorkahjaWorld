# Frostforged Vault

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Ancient sealed vault  
**Region:** [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md)

The Frostforged Vault is an ancient sealed Frostweaver vault. Its lock is a challenge in itself, preserving whatever memory, relic, or danger the Frostweavers left within.

## Relic Traditions

- Everkey of Frostweaver's Memory - key associated with opening the vault.

## The Sealed Door

The vault's great door bears intricate icy patterns attributed to Frostweaver craft. Its surroundings are unusually cold. Distant sounds resembling ritual chants are reported in the wind, but the contents of the vault remain unknown.
