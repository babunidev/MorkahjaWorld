# Evergrove

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) heartland  
**Associated people:** [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md)  
**Known features:** Desert realm, oasis-gardens, salt flats, wind-cut mesas, hydromantic canals, Memory Pools, root-vein systems

Evergrove is the Verdani heartland, a vast desert realm of dunes, salt flats, wind-cut mesas, endless heat, and cultivated oasis-gardens. Outsiders often mistake it for a contradiction, believing nothing should live there. The Verdani see clarity.

Here, water exists not as abundance, but as intention. Through hydromancy, healing, and song, the Verdani guide water and growth into shimmering places of refuge.

At its center lies the Evergrove Nexus, a living root-vein lattice beneath layers of sandstone and mineral shelves. This immense network of water-bearing roots, living channels, mineral arteries, and magical ecosystems stretches beneath the sands. Verdani mythology claims these roots formed where Nereval first learned stillness, while hydromancers claim the network is partially engineered.

Without the Nexus, Evergrove would die.

Settlements spread outward from this underground system like blossoms from buried roots. Oasis Hearts serve as local anchors, grown from Nexus offshoots and expanded over generations through ecological engineering and hydromantic cultivation.

Verdani cities rarely resemble conventional settlements. Buildings curve around water flow rather than forcing it elsewhere. Sandstone, saltstone, polished clay, living wood, and cultivated root systems blend into structures that appear partially grown rather than constructed. Canals weave through nearly every settlement, and water is visible everywhere: controlled, guided, and celebrated.

Transportation across Evergrove follows the logic of flow. Caravan routes connect distant settlements through mapped oasis chains, while hydromantic canal systems allow movement of goods and travelers between larger population centers. Currentwalkers navigate underground waterways and Nexus routes few outsiders understand.

## Open Possibilities

- Oasis-gardens can serve as healing sites, pilgrimage destinations, or diplomatic meeting grounds.
- Evergrove is a heartland of Verdani birth, memory, and ecological stewardship.
- Verdani stewardship here connects strongly to Nereval, Sylvanus, Aerisara, and the living currents of the Abyssal Conclave.
- The Great Quiet and the Drying Wars may have left hidden scars in Evergrove's oldest waters.
- Drought cycles, root sickness, saltstorms, disrupted water veins, and collapsing Oasis Hearts are major hazards.
- Currentwalkers can lead travelers through hidden Nexus routes.

## Altar of Umbrael

A modest cave in Evergrove contains an altar marked with Umbrael's crescent moon. Candlelight, shadow, and quiet support meditation on secrets, intuition, and hidden truths. The shrine is a concrete example of worship extending beyond a people's primary tribunal.
