# Eternal Twilight Grove

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Haunted forest  
**Known feature:** Restless spirits

The Eternal Twilight Grove is a haunted forest where spirits may be calmed through music or magic. Its twilight atmosphere suggests a place caught between memory, grief, and renewal.

## Relic Traditions

- Moonchime Harp - item used to calm spirits through music or magic.

## The Spectral Chorus

Luminous leaves shimmer beneath a perpetually starlit sky. Restless spirits move through the grove's cold mist, their laments mingling with the wind. Music and calming magic can quiet the chorus, revealing a gentler beauty beneath its distress.
