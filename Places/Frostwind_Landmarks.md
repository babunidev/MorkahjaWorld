# Frostwind Landmarks

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Frostwind Junkyard

A concealed shop occupies a rocky crevice in the [Frostwind Mountains](Frostwind_Mountains.md). A reserved [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) proprietor sorts old runes, relics, potions, and unusual objects by torchlight. Collectors know it for its varied stock and the owner's shrewd judgment.

## Cliffs of Sarga

Deep crevices cut tall, weathered cliffs. Wind passing through their chasms produces a resonant, almost musical sound. Hidden cave treasures are a local tradition. Their exact position relative to the Frostwind range remains unresolved.

## Mountain Pass

An old high route winds between icy peaks above the cloudline. Sharp winds, exposed stone, and narrow turns make travel difficult. Tales of a concealed hoard accompany the pass, but neither its owners nor its resting place are known.

## Frostwind Slopes

Snow, freezing mist, ice-crusted rivers, and jagged trails define the slopes. Hidden caves and deep drifts preserve old stories as well as physical dangers. Sudden weather changes can erase visibility across an otherwise open landscape.

## Strange Boulder

A moss-marked boulder rests near a mountain summit above a green valley. Accounts mention an unusual fragrance at its base and an impression of hidden magic. Its exact position and origin remain unidentified.

## The Giant

The Giant is a solitary rock monolith rising vertically from a steep mountainside. Changing light can make its weathered form seem animate. Its name describes its scale and silhouette; it is not established as a living giant.

## Bronze Vault

A heavily locked bronze vault stands in the Frostwind Mountains. Complex etchings cover its surfaces, but no makers have been identified. Its position and imposing construction have encouraged stories of a forgotten people.

## Frost Strider

A great rock formation resembles a titan paused in mid-stride. Weather has sharpened its outline with frost and snow. Some believe it can move or that it is an ancient golem; neither claim is established.
