# Everwinter Cave

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Icy cave  
**Region:** [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md)

Everwinter Cave lies behind a troll-guarded entrance. Within are ice statues, frozen fossils, ancient runes, and hidden treasure.

## The Guardian

- [White Ice Troll Guardian](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#everwinter-guardian) is another name for the [Everwinter Guardian](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#everwinter-guardian), the ice-blue troll watching the cave entrance.

## Relic Traditions

- Frostberry Banquet is a provision associated with the guardian; its role in local hospitality remains to be developed.
- Frostweaver Resonance Compass is associated with the cave in older notes; its precise identity remains unresolved.

## Fossils and Frost

Clear ice encloses prehistoric fossils within the cavern walls. Large stalactites hang above frozen sculptures and inscriptions in an unknown script. The relationship between the natural formations and worked figures has not been established.
