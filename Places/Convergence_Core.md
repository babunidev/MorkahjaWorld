# Convergence Core

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) Skyland center  
**Associated people:** [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md)  
**Parent location:** [Grimnir Skylands](Grimnir_Skylands.md)

The Convergence Core is an innovative center within Grimnir civilization, where Skyland development accelerates into forms that sometimes resemble living machines of stone and light.

It appears to be a place of heightened experimentation, dynamic architecture, and advanced resonance craft. Whether it is a single Skyland, a recurring alignment of multiple Skylands, or a permanent political-cultural center remains open for development.

## Open Possibilities

- A home for Expansionist philosophy and experimental architecture.
- A site where Skylands may gather for major decisions, trade, or technological advancement.
- A possible source of unstable innovation or living stone-machine constructs.
