# Eldertree Landmarks

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Eldertree Grove

A great ancient tree forms the center of the grove. Legends say the first giants carved signs of their culture into its bark and that [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) gathered around it for songs of water and life. Those claims belong to the grove's traditions, not a settled account of the world's creation.

## Shadowed Hollow

A cave hidden within the forest is associated with old mining and spell-weaving tales. Its walls are said to conceal precious metals and gems, while stories place fae gatherings in its darkness. The identities and age of its earliest occupants remain uncertain.

## Sunlit Dell

A quiet clearing is remembered as a place for communal song, dance, and deliberation. Some old stories place giants among its visitors. Its warmth and natural shelter make it a place of recovery within the forest.

## Enchanted Hollow

A hidden forest den is said to shelter powerful fae who guard ancient knowledge. Their identity and the scale of their power are uncertain. The hollow's reputation belongs as much to woodland storytelling as to reliable observation.

## Sylvan Sanctuary

A natural refuge opens in the side of a wooded cliff. Moss gives the walls a gentle light, and a nearby stream can be heard from within. Its name does not establish a dedication to Sylvanus; it is first known as a sheltered place away from prying eyes.

## Tranquil Glade

Tall trees shelter a small, clear stream. Pine, wildflowers, insects, and dappled sunlight make the glade a natural resting place. It is also described as a forest idyll, rather than as a separate settlement.

## Feral Hollow

Dense growth conceals a moss-lined woodland lair. Pine needles cover its floor, and sheltered recesses offer warmth and concealment. Its quiet is broken mainly by passing animals and the movement of leaves.

## Moonlit Oasis

A woodland pond glows softly after nightfall. Floating lights, luminous ferns, and fireflies gather around the still water, while ducks nest along its edge. Its name does not establish that it belongs to [Evergrove](Evergrove.md)'s desert oasis network.

## Grimnir Steps

Small stairs hewn from natural rock climb a gentle forest incline. One tradition says they gave underground [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) a quiet route to the surface. Their date and makers are uncertain, and the tale does not define the homeland of all Grimnir.

## Firefly Brook

Clear water passes over smooth pebbles beneath drifting fireflies. Local accounts speak of protective spirits whose whispers mingle with the stream. Travelers use the brook as a quiet resting place amid the active nighttime forest.

## Verdani Treasury

The Treasury is a modest shop within [Eldertree Forest](Eldertree_Forest.md), its wood and thatch wrapped in vines and moss. An elderly Verdani binds books, offers tea, and sells talismans, potions, herbs, and small crafted goods. It is a place of conversation and inherited knowledge, not an established state treasury.

## Celestial Cairn

A stone guardian watches over a woodland stream crossed by small, water-smoothed platforms. The cairn is a place of meditation and learning within Eldertree Forest. No direct dedication to Aetheria has been established.
