# Aevis Subterranean Cities

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Underground city network  
**Associated people:** [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md)  
**Primary city:** [Celesterra](Celesterra.md)  
**Known features:** Crystal channels, stone spires, guided air currents, pressure systems, resonance platforms

The [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) dwell in vast subterranean cities where air is carefully guided through crystal channels, pressure corridors, resonance platforms, and stone spires. These cities reflect Aevis mastery of magic, architecture, and thought, turning the underground into a refined environment of controlled movement and disciplined grace.

Their homeland is not a nation in the traditional sense. It is an atmosphere engineered into civilization. [Celesterra](Celesterra.md) is the oldest and largest of these hidden sky-cities, but countless smaller settlements extend through mountain roots, deep caverns, and interconnected pressure systems beneath ancient continents.

Though the Aevis are wind-born, their civilization is rooted beneath the surface. Their atmospheric craft subtly supports the [Grimnir Skylands](Grimnir_Skylands.md) above Celesterra, creating an unspoken equilibrium between the lower air-shapers and the upper stone-forgers.

## Common Structures

- Resonance lift platforms.
- Airflow highways.
- Vertical spiral corridors.
- Bridge systems between stone towers and cavern walls.
- Atmospheric gates.
- Resonance chambers.
- Pressure sanctuaries.

## Open Possibilities

- The cities may control or influence air currents that sustain Skyland stability.
- Crystal channels and resonance chambers function as both infrastructure and magical instruments.
- Deep subterranean resonance may be uncomfortable or dangerous to [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) identity coherence.
- Airflow collapse events and resonance instability zones can threaten entire districts.
