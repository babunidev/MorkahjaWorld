# Frostfall Cascade

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Frozen waterfall  
**Region:** [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md)

Frostfall Cascade is a frozen waterfall in [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md). A strange mysterious object is visible halfway up the ice, tempting climbers into danger.

## Relic Traditions

- [Glacier Grapnel](../Objects/Weapons_and_Tools.md#glacier-grapnel) - climbing tool associated with reaching the object.

## The Shape within the Ice

A glint or shadow remains visible partway up the frozen waterfall. Songs and local stories mention it without agreeing on its nature. The steep, slippery ice makes close examination difficult.
