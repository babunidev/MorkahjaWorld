# Frostweaver Palace Ruins

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Monumental ancient ruins  
**Region:** [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md)

The Frostweaver Palace Ruins are monumental remnants of an ancient Frostweaver seat of power. Inscription tables within the ruins preserve lost knowledge and practical teachings.
