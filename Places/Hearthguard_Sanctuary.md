# Hearthguard Sanctuary

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## The Hearth Chamber

Carved into the earth, the sanctuary offers an eternal hearth, earthen cooking vessels, and a stone bench polished by generations of use. Its fire does not diminish. A magical barrier closes around the entrance, sheltering those within from weather and unwanted attention.

## Mystic Hearth's Seal

The refuge's approach lies among tall stones in northern [Ethera's Vale](Etheras_Vale.md). An elemental barrier protects its entrance, requiring an understanding of the magic that holds it. Mystic Hearth's Seal names that threshold rather than a separate town or region.
