# Pantheon Temple

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## The Temple Halls

A temple south of Eldridia, near [Eldertree Forest](Eldertree_Forest.md), welcomes worshippers of the Pantheon. Divine symbols glow upon its architecture; quiet chants and sacred herbs accompany meditation and magical study. Its precise position is unresolved, and it has not been established as one of [Aetherion](Aetherion.md)'s city shrines.
