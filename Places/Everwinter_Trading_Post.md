# Everwinter Trading Post

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Southern outpost  
**Region:** [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md)

The Everwinter Trading Post is a southern outpost on the edge of [Everwinter Valley](Everwinter_Valley.md). It is operated by a beautiful young [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) woman and serves as a lifeline for travelers entering the permafrost.

Fresh food is always in demand here, making supply, trade, and survival tightly connected.

## Open Possibilities

- The trading post may be the safest first refuge before deeper Everwinter exploration.
- Fresh food can function as currency or diplomatic leverage.
- Its Rokur operator may connect Everwinter to fire, warmth, and outsider trade.

## The Hearth and the Keeper

A Rokur woman keeps the southern trading post warm and supplied. Fur-lined clothing and preserved provisions line its shelves, while stew simmers by the fire. Fresh food is especially valued in the frozen valley.
