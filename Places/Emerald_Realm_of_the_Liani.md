# Emerald Realm of the Liani

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Location:** Deep within [Eldertree Forest](Eldertree_Forest.md)  
**Classification:** Liani refuge, hidden woodland realm  
**Elemental reading:** Nature, Spirit, Water, Growth, living memory

The Emerald Realm is a secluded domain within the oldest reaches of [Eldertree Forest](Eldertree_Forest.md). It is inhabited by the Liani, whose relationship with the living woodland shapes the realm's paths, clearings, and protective boundaries.

The realm is not simply a settlement hidden among trees. It is a place where forest and community are treated as parts of one living system. Streams, roots, animal paths, and cultivated clearings are maintained through cooperation rather than imposed design.

Those permitted to enter may find sanctuaries, ancient groves, and paths that respond to memory or intention. Outsiders rarely agree on its size or layout. Some claim that the realm changes with the seasons; others believe it reveals only the part of itself that a visitor is prepared to understand.

## Open Possibilities

- The Liani may test visitors before revealing the realm's oldest knowledge.
- The Emerald Realm may contain a surviving record of ancient natural cycles.
- Liani guardians may know whether [Keldor](Keldor.md)'s corruption began as a natural wound, magical disaster, or older intrusion.
