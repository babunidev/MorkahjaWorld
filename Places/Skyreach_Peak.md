# Skyreach Peak

[Places index](Places_Index.md) · [All lore](../../README.md)

**Type:** Mountain summit  
**Region:** [Frostwind Mountains](Frostwind_Mountains.md)

Skyreach Peak is the highest point in the [Frostwind Mountains](Frostwind_Mountains.md). Its ascent is treacherous, and Frostweaver relics are rumored to lie at the summit.

## Relic Traditions

- Frostweaver's Grappling Hook - climbing tool associated with the ascent.

## The Summit Path

Skyreach is the highest point of the Frostwind Mountains. Icy ridges and narrow ledges climb into cloud, where sudden storms can obscure the route. Frostweaver relics are rumored to survive near the summit.
