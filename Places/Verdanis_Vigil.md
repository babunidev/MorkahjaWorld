## The Lazaret

At the edge of [Keldor](Keldor.md), a small lazaret shelters people recovered from the forest. Wooden shutters admit morning light over medicinal plants, tied herbs, and glowing vials. A [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) healer treats trauma and exhaustion with water magic beneath protective wards. Survivors' memories of roots and impossible structures are often confused with delirium; the healer does not confirm every account.
