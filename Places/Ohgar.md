# Ohgar

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## The Inscribed Temple Disc

A ruined temple in Ohgar contains a large stone disc raised upon a pedestal. Weathered runes record an old philosophy that remains only partly understood. Stone steps lead to the disc, where visitors meditate and study beneath the open desert sky.

## Rocks of Ohgar

Granite monoliths rise over the desert, their faces cut by wind and heat. Groaning sounds are reported among their crevices during intense sun and wind. Stories place hidden chambers or treasures inside the formations.
