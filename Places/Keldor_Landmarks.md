# Keldor Landmarks

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Darkwood Thicket

Twisted trees and old leaf litter enclose a western woodland associated with [Keldor](Keldor.md). A local tale says its fae inhabitants were once banished from Morkahja. Strange creatures and whispering winds remain, but the truth and cause of that banishment are unknown.

## Luminous Enclave

A luminous wooden hut resists the corruption surrounding it. Moss and grass cover the walls, while magical curios fill its interior. Its unnamed hag proprietor is remembered for humor and hospitality amid the forest's dangers.

## Twilight Whisper Grove

Perpetual fog and faint lights drift among rotting stumps and gnarled trees. Humidity and decay fill the air. Travelers describe whispers and a confused sense of past and present within the grove.

## Enigma's End

A path through Keldor appears to end in blue-white light. Fog, misleading shadows, and illusions make approach uncertain. The light's source and whether it marks a safe destination are unknown.

## Path of Despair

Dead trees and grey ground enclose a route associated with overwhelming hopelessness. Shapes resembling skeletal remains may be real or deceptive. The path's oppressive influence is part of Keldor's disruption of perception and resolve.

## Gloomroot Expanse

Great dead roots form a hazardous maze, some resembling distorted faces. Poisonous gases emerge from the earth. The dangers of this region include both physical contamination and the disorienting effects of the forest's magic.

## Wraithbone Pathway

Bones and skulls lie beside a narrow trail beneath dead branches and thick fog. The remains are associated with forgotten conflicts. Whether the land can recover from its curse is unknown.

## Skullstone Pass

Jagged, skull-like rocks border a dark corridor through Keldor. Dense fog and a foul smell make passage difficult. Dead trees and steep stone create hazards above the path as well as along it.

## Sageglow Clearing

A [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) scholar studies by the light of luminous fireflies in this sheltered clearing. His calm presence offers a rare contrast to Keldor's oppressive atmosphere. His name and the permanence of the clearing's protection are unknown.

## Sylvan Rune Gateway

An old stone doorway in southern Keldor is bound into the roots and limbs of a great tree. Faces and unreadable script cover its frame. A magical lock protects it; whether it leads to a chamber or another realm is unresolved.
