# Vale Landmarks

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Lovely Meadows

Rocky meadows, wild herbs, and flowers surround large moss-covered stones. Birds and small animals share the uneven ground with larger creatures. Old magic can be felt beneath the greenery, and local stories place forgotten treasures under the rocks.

## Enchanted Glades

Tall trees and high grasses cover hills crossed by a clear stream. Deer and foxes move among wildflowers and old stone pillars. The glades lie within [Ethera's Vale](Etheras_Vale.md) and are known for their tranquility and lingering magical power.

## Fae Pastures

A deep valley of tall grass and rich wild growth surrounds vine-covered stone pillars. Stories associate the valley with mischievous fae and suggest that the pillars, or the valley itself, offer passage into hidden realms. The nature of those realms is unresolved.

## Mystic Grass

Tall grass covers rolling hills around a great pillar standing upon a mound of rocks. Those sensitive to elemental currents describe a strong pulse beneath the soil. Traditions disagree over whether the pillar marks buried knowledge or a passage beyond the familiar world.

## Seraphic Plains

Rough-hewn pillars rise from a broad sea of tall grass. Their cracked faces and apparent arrangement give the impression of ancient boundary markers. The plains carry an unusual balance of natural and magical forces; their pillars' original purpose is unknown.

## Fey Greens

Deer and stags gather around a sanctuary built into a great upright rock. The refuge is associated with [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) care and respect for the land. Its peaceful appearance coexists with strong magic and warnings against treating the landscape carelessly.

## Celestial Fields

Broad summer fields hold wildflowers, butterflies, and abundant small life. Warm currents and the hum of insects give the place its serene character. Stories of older secrets beneath the flowers persist without identifying their origin.

## Enigmatic Prairies

Travelers report subtle shifts in the terrain of these rolling grasslands, as though paths and distances respond to the magic beneath them. An unidentified powerful artifact is rumored to lie within their interior. Neither its nature nor the cause of the shifting land is established.

## Arcane Meadows

Rolling hills and bright vegetation extend through the northeastern part of Ethera's Vale. Long shadows and gentle tree movement give the meadows a quiet, enchanted character. Stories mention hidden treasures without a confirmed site.

## Tranquil Wilds

Golden fields stretch among trees with ochre and yellow leaves. Wind moves through grass and foliage with a soft, continuous rustle. The remembered landscape is pastoral and warm; its seasonal changes remain undescribed.

## Dreamy Valley

Warm meadows of pink flowers lie among large rocks. Visitors describe an unusual sense of anticipation as they move deeper into the valley. The source of this enchantment is unknown.

## Arcadian Hills

Lush hills and flowered meadows are associated with enchantment and stories of good fortune for respectful visitors. A borderstone bears the letter A. The maker and meaning of the mark remain unknown.

## Alluring Fens

Mist hangs over shallow pools and tall reeds. Trees rise from black water, their branches draped with moss, while fireflies drift through the fog. Unexplained singing is reported at night. The fens' precise boundaries within the central lands remain open.

## Enchanted Gardens

Rugged stones rise among fragrant wildflowers. At twilight, unfamiliar creature calls and concentrated magic give the gardens an unsettling character. One rock bears the letter H; its significance is unknown.

## Mystical Vale

Emerald fields, open meadows, and gentle hills give this small vale an expansive calm. Birdsong and the scent of flowers dominate its atmosphere. A large boulder bears the letter K.

## Bucolic Dale

A sheltered dale of wildflowers and active wildlife lies among rolling hills. Warm light lingers across the landscape at evening. One stone carries the letter J, whose origin has not been explained.

## Captivating Downs

Tall grass and bright flowers cover a landscape associated with old magic. Tales describe a thin boundary with the fae realms, though this has not been confirmed. A nearby rock bears the letter O.

## Radiant Heath

An open heath carries a warm, luminous atmosphere among wild grasses and flowers. A stone is engraved with the letter M. Accounts of the [Radiant Quill](../Objects/Relics_and_Enigmas.md#radiant-quill) also associate the heath with glowing waters.

## Mesmerizing Hillsides

Green slopes receive long, warm sunlight, with wind moving through diverse flowers and grasses. Local descriptions emphasize their calming atmosphere and persistent enchantment rather than a settlement or monument.

## Magical Glens

Light and concentrated magic fill these wooded glens. Stories speak of a thin boundary between worlds and secrets held beneath the soil. A rock marked A belongs to the scattered inscribed stones of the central lands; their relationship remains unknown.

## Ethereal Glades

Bright foliage and a quiet, charged atmosphere give these glades an otherworldly character. A thin boundary with unseen realms is reported but not explained. Their relationship to the nearby Magical Glens remains unestablished.
