# MorkahjaWorld — Design

**Creative:** Babuni

## World design

Morkahja is a young, elemental fantasy world. Its existing [World Identity](../Overview/World_Identity.md) describes Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and the connecting nature of Aether, alongside wonder, danger, mystery, and the consequences of magic's corruption.

My world-building material is arranged as interrelated accounts of places, races, creatures, culture, objects, and history. The archive preserves unresolved possibilities where the world has deliberately been left open; documentation maintenance must not turn every mystery into a settled answer.

## Presentation design

The repository currently presents this material as **linked Markdown files** with subject indexes and cross-references. It does not contain a verified website interface, game screens, visual assets, or an implemented interaction design for MorkahjaWorld.

The repository's documentation therefore records the *design of the world and its written organization*, not a fictional software product.

## Future changes

New visuals, gameplay, or presentation conventions should be documented only when their direction has been decided and their files or implementation can be traced. Material in other Babuni repositories must not be silently described as adopted here.
