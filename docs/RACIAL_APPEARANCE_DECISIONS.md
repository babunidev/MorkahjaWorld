# Morkahja — Racial Appearance Decisions

**Creative:** Babuni

**Status:** Ongoing creative review; confirmed decisions only.

To give the four peoples a clearer visual identity, I am defining their shared anatomy and racial distinctions while preserving individual variation. The Verdani in particular should retain broad variation reflecting their living, growing and changing elemental nature, within boundaries still to be defined.

Each numbered heading corresponds to a question in the review. Only confirmed answers are recorded here. This record establishes the approved direction; integration into individual lore entries and game content is separate work.

## Shared racial anatomy

### 01 — Humanoid anatomy

All four peoples are fundamentally humanoid, with familiar human-like proportions and room for individual and racial differences. Their form reflects the original humanoid shape of their divine makers, whose essence shapes the peoples they brought forth.

The gods have ascended beyond a fixed physical form and can choose their appearance. Although they commonly manifest as dragons, the humanoid form remains their original and ideal self-image. This does not revise the established rules governing their physical intervention in the world.

The peoples share a humanoid external anatomy, but their biological functions differ according to their respective elemental natures.

### 02 — Height and general build

Natural adult height ranges remain within familiar human dimensions, with distinct tendencies for each people.

| People | Adult height | General build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verdani | 140–165 cm | Petite, slender |
| Grimnir | 160–175 cm | Comparatively short, very strongly built |
| Rokur | 170–185 cm | Tall, within familiar human proportions |
| Aevis | 182–200 cm | Tallest of the four peoples |

The Verdani are generally the smallest and the Aevis the tallest. These ranges describe natural adult stature; detailed proportions and the treatment of individual exceptions remain open.

### 03 — Body proportions

The four peoples have distinct natural physiques within their shared humanoid anatomy. These are characteristic proportions rather than a requirement for every individual to look identical.

| People | Characteristic proportions |
| --- | --- |
| Grimnir | Broad shoulders, thick limbs, substantial hands and a compact torso. |
| Verdani | Petite frames, slender limbs, narrow shoulders and delicate features. |
| Rokur | Athletic, muscular bodies with strong shoulders and balanced human proportions. |
| Aevis | Long limbs, elongated necks, slender torsos and elegant proportions. |
