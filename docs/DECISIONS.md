## 15 November 2026 — Establish the Pantheon's shared laws

Through a question-by-question review of my twelve-deity Pantheon, I chose to **keep all four tribunals and all twelve gods** rather than introduce deities for every human concern. The following decisions were explicitly approved:

- **Essence and death:** Every living being is sustained by divine essence that can be gained, given, or taken. Death comes with the loss of the last life-sustaining fragment, which returns to its source deity. The enduring soul passes into Atranor.
- **Judgment and resurrection:** Aerisara, Sylvanus, and the deity of the last essence hold the central judgment. Resurrection returns the being to its previous life and memories, and requires at least one willing god to give essence. No deity is obliged to give it, and the gift need not come from all twelve.
- **Reincarnation:** Every soul has the unconditional right to begin another life, unanimously upheld by the gods. A deity cannot imprison a soul within its inner realm. A soul may remain in its associated realm while retaining full individuality, or choose a new mortal form and people. Multiple deities share the essence that sustains the new incarnation; conscious memories are lost, though traces of former mortal lives may remain.
- **Divine realms:** Every deity has a distinct inner realm. The circumstances and experiences inside them remain deliberately unknowable, and are not recovered in reincarnation.
- **Time and destiny:** Ordius and Khaotys jointly keep time through continuity and change. The future is not predetermined; neither gods nor mortals can see what has not yet occurred.
- **Divine nature:** The gods are inseparable from the world, indestructible but capable of extremely slow change. They cooperate without rival agendas. In an unmanifested state they may give or take essence; direct physical intervention requires their dragon forms. Appearances are occasional, approximately as common as holidays, not fixed festival appointments.
- **Shared mortal domains:** Aerisara/Fyraan share love; Umbrael/Aetheria dreams; Sylvanus/Khaotys decay; Fyraan/Ferrumis war; Ordius/Nereval peace; Umbrael/Aerisara memory; Fyraan/Aetheria creativity; Khaotys/Ordius luck; Aeriel/Nereval travel.

**Superseded account:** The former [Atranor](../Places/Atranor.md) passage sending souls denied resurrection into the Aether with uncertain survival no longer expresses the approved universal cycle. It is replaced by the deity's inner realm, free choice, and the unconditional right to reincarnate. The earlier account survives in Git history. The diverse beliefs of Rokur, Verdani, Aevis, and Grimnir remain their peoples' interpretations, not overrides of the shared divine law.

**Intentionally open:** The interior experience of divine realms, whether departed souls meet there, the precise physical beginning of a newly chosen life, and the mechanics of the Dice of Fortune remain unresolved. These were not permission to invent answers.

## 2026-11-26 — Eternal Twilight Grove belongs to Eldertree Forest

**Decision (Babuni):** the Eternal Twilight Grove is part of Eldertree Forest. Its spirits and its tradition of calming them through music or magic stay as written. **Open:** its exact place inside the forest. **Recorded by:** Claude Code (Claude Opus 5.5).

## 2026-11-26 — The Grimnir Vault lies in Celesterra

**Decision (Babuni):** the Grimnir Vault is in the mountains of Celesterra, the Grimnir homeland below their Skylands, not in Deepstone Hollow near Everwinter. The Verdani Casket lies in Eldertree Forest. **Open:** who sealed the vault and what it keeps. **Recorded by:** Claude Code (Claude Opus 5.5).
