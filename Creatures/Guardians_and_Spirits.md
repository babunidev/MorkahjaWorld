# Guardians and Spirits

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Blackwood Guardian

An ancient black tree guards passage within the [Blackwood Abyss](../Places/Blackwood_Abyss.md). Its roots move to entangle intruders. Accounts mention a password or an object by which it recognizes a permitted visitor, but no reliable condition of passage has been established.

## Veilwalker

A tentacled being with apparently suspended, lidless eyes is reported in the eastern meadows of [Ethera's Vale](../Places/Etheras_Vale.md). Its presence troubles nearby villagers. Its origin and any relationship to [Keldor](../Places/Keldor.md)'s tentacled creatures remain unknown.
