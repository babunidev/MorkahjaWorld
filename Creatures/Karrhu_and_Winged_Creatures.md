# Elemental Beasts

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Ohgar Skorpo

The Ohgar Skorpo is a desert scorpion protected by a dark brown exoskeleton. Two strong pincers and a curved, venomous tail allow it to hunt and defend itself among dunes and sparse scrub. It uses scattered cover and is sensitive to movement around it.

## Inferno Duck

An iridescent bird of [Karrhu](../Places/Karrhu.md)'s wetlands spits searing magical fire. One tale attributes its power to a sorcerer, but its origin is unknown. Its appearance gives little warning of the danger of its flame.

## Zeelai

Zeelai are reported as shapeshifting beings that consume life force. One known manifestation takes a powerful feline shape and radiates unstable arcane energy. Their origin and full range of forms remain unknown.

## Thunderfly Matriarch

A large sapphire-and-silver winged creature flies above the Liani woods at dusk. Luminous chitin covers its body, and its antennae respond to electrical charge. Thunderous wingbeats and storm energy make the Matriarch a striking expression of elemental life.

## Frostwind Spiders

The silken threads of Frostwind spiders are used to weave the [Frostweave Cloak](../Objects/Clothing_and_Protection.md#frostweave-cloak). Their size, behavior, and precise habitat remain undescribed.
