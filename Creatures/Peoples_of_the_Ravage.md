## Ogrus beyond the Warbands

An account from eastern [Ethera's Vale](../Places/Etheras_Vale.md) describes a large grey Ogru sleeping beneath a willow, its body resembling a boulder among the roots. Such sightings place Ogrus in ordinary periods of rest as well as in the violence of the Ravage. A sleeping individual is not itself evidence of an imminent awakening.
