# Nagar

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Bloodfang

The Bloodfang is a powerful Nagar boar recognized by sharp projecting horns and a formidable charge. Its growl and heavy movement often silence nearby wildlife. Whether Bloodfang names a particular beast or a recurring form remains uncertain.

## Goldhorn

Goldhorn is a horned wild boar known for great strength and an intimidating charge. Accounts emphasize its resilience and the tremor of its approach. Its exact relationship to other named Nagar forms is not fully described.

## Nagar Charger

A large but fast Nagar of [Ethera's Vale](../Places/Etheras_Vale.md), the Charger carries massive tusks and a muscular body. Its defining danger is a rapid, sustained charge across ground that shakes beneath its hooves.

## Nagar Elder

An aged, scarred Nagar combines considerable size with the caution and cunning of long survival. Its bristled hide is weathered and its tusks bear marks of conflict. The title need not imply a formal rank among Nagar.

## Nagar Berserk

The name is applied to a Nagar in a state of extreme rage, charging with enough weight to shake the ground. It need not denote a separate lineage or species.

## Nagar Boar

Nagar are enormous wild boars of Ethera's Vale, with curling tusks, powerful bodies, and grey-brown hides that blend with undergrowth. Accounts describe considerable animal intelligence and formidable territorial aggression.

## Bonecrusher

A Nagar variant known for a scarred, bone-white hide and crimson eyes. Its pointed horns are reputed to break shields and bones. Whether Bonecrusher is a singular beast or a name shared by similar animals remains unknown.

## Nagar Humpback

A large Nagar distinguished by a pronounced hump and heavy tusks. Its physical strength and weight make it dangerous when it charges across the Vale.

## Nagar Razorback

Razorbacks bear sharp horn-like projections along the spine. These add to the danger posed by the animal's charge and make close pursuit hazardous.

## Thunderhoof

The Thunderhoof is a colossal horned Nagar recognized by the heavy, repeated impact of its hooves. Its approach can be felt through the ground. Tales of its ability to crush obstacles reflect both its real mass and its reputation among hunters.
