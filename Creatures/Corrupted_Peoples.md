# Corrupted Peoples

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Ashen Revenant Witch

An Ashen Revenant witch carries poisonous green magic around a charred, cracked body. Her spells produce a choking miasma and cause nearby growth to wither. The visible energies can resemble flowing garments around her ruined form.

## Ashen Revenant Seer

A seer is described with corrupt flame enclosed in a brittle blackened shell. Hot, dark vapor spreads around the figure, and spoken prophecies accompany intensifying fire. These accounts concern a manifestation of corrupted [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) life, not a separate people.

## Blightwraith Huntress

This predatory Blightwraith drains life from the plants around her while stalking through woodland. Corruption has distorted both her body and her relationship with living growth, leaving decay along her passage.

## Crumblegheist Mage

A Crumblegheist mage associated with the Shattered Peaks guards a dark cave. Patterned leather armor and a broad green hat preserve traces of former craft and identity beneath the corruption. The figure gathers unstable magic in one hand; the cave's contents remain unknown.

## Tempestwailer Wildheart

The Wildheart summons storms with fierce emotional conviction. Corruption has distorted her judgment so that she understands destructive acts as righteous ones. Her red hair and defiant bearing distinguish this account from other Tempestwailer sightings.

## Blightwraith Rootrot

Rootrot is a severely distorted Blightwraith whose former sentient shape is barely recognizable. Its passage drains trees and fills the air with decay. The name describes the destructive form by which it is recognized.

## Crumblegheist Berserk

A heavily armored Crumblegheist rages through mountain terrain, breaking stone underfoot. Deep fissures expose the emptiness within its formerly coherent mineral body. Spiked steel armor remains part of its threatening silhouette.

## Crumblegheist Barbarian

A red-haired Crumblegheist in heavy, darkly enchanted armor is described among broken rocks. Her movements bring tremors and further erosion. The title refers to this violent manifestation rather than an established [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) social class.

## Tempestwailer Stormcaller

The Stormcaller moves through dark woodland in rough blue and brown robes. Lightning gathers around her hands as thunder and dense storm-clouds accompany her incantations. Her influence makes the surrounding atmosphere unstable.

## Tempestwailer Seductress

An alluring Tempestwailer uses enchantment and disorienting storm magic to draw others close. Fine green and gold robes veil a corrupted form. The danger lies in her manipulation of perception as much as in the turbulent air around her.

## Blightwraith Occultist

The Occultist practices rites that drain a forest's life through tendrils of dark energy. Gnarled horns and a body fused with distorted armor mark the transformation. Roots and branches wither as the rite draws strength from them.

## Ashen Revenant Warlock

A warlock among scorched ruins bears cracks through which molten and oil-dark substances pulse. Arcane signs gather around his gestures. The surviving outline of beard, clothing, and deliberate movement suggests a former identity beneath the consuming corruption.

## Emberlost Wraith

A tormented Ashen Revenant wanders [Keldor](../Places/Keldor.md), setting rotten trees alight as she passes. Her subdued movements suggest a will suppressed by the forest's magic. Smoke and collapsing wood follow her through the shadows.

## Flamecrazed Wanderer

An Ashen Revenant leaves burning footprints and hums a dissonant tune among Keldor's dead trees. Her movements are unpredictable, and accounts describe her as driven by the surrounding corruption. Her former identity is unknown.

## Gloomthorn Sentinel

A Blightwraith remains motionless among twisted trees until nearby life draws its attention. Jagged horns and purple eyes mark its outline. Once moving, it spreads a withering magical haze.

## Stormshriek Harbinger

A corrupted [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) is enveloped in dark cloud and lashes the forest with lightning and violent wind. Claw-like hands and a fragmented airy form remain visible within the storm. The name records its destructive manifestation, not a new elemental people.
