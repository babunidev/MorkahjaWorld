# Wolves and Liani

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Liani Shadowwolf

The seldom-seen Shadowwolf is associated with hidden knowledge and tests of worthiness. Accounts place its counsel beyond ordinary understanding. Its relationship to the cursed Liani of [Keldor](../Places/Keldor.md) and to other Liani remains unresolved.

## Silvermane

Silvermane names a formidable silver-furred predator associated with the wilderness. Reports emphasize great strength, sharp claws, and powerful jaws. Its relationship to Silver Wolves and to Keldor's cursed Silvermane has not been fully established.

## Yowler

A medium-sized wolf with mottled grey and brown fur, the Yowler is known for calls that carry far across a valley. It often howls from rocky outcrops, unnerving larger animals and advertising its presence.

## Sarga Lioness

A tawny mountain lioness is associated with Sarga's rocky crevices. Keen green eyes, muscular strength, and precise footing make her well adapted to the cliffs. The accounts do not establish a magical origin.

## Silver Wolf

Silver Wolves inhabit the northern Vale and southeastern approaches to Keldor. Moonlight catches their sleek silver coats, while strong muscles and rapid movement make them effective predators. Their relation to the named Silvermane remains open.

## Shena Alpha

A glossy-coated pack leader is reported near the boundary of Keldor and [Ethera's Vale](../Places/Etheras_Vale.md). The Shena Alpha displays unusual vigilance and intelligence. The name does not yet establish whether Shena denotes a distinct kind of wolf.

## Embersnout

A largely solitary Frostwind wolf with a thick, fiery-colored coat. Strong scent-tracking and quick footing allow it to pursue prey across ice and snow. Its coloration does not by itself imply fire magic.

## Meadow Wolves

Grey-brown meadow wolves conceal their dens and young in tall grass. Females guarding cubs can react fiercely to an approach. Their camouflage and vigilance are ordinary parts of protecting a den.

## Redeye

A young wolf called a Redeye is described with crimson eyes and a thick russet coat. Its curiosity and coloring suit the meadow account in which it appears. The adult form and broader range of Redeyes are unknown.

## Cursed Silvermane

Keldor accounts describe a large silver-coated wolf afflicted by spectral magic. Matted patches, unnaturally bright eyes, and a disturbing aura distinguish it from an ordinary predator. The origin of the curse remains unknown.

## Cursed Liani

A shadow-dark Liani with cyan eyes is reported moving silently through Keldor. Its fur seems to absorb light, and its passing leaves a chill and a mournful howl. It is sometimes called a Shadowwolf, but a single identity linking every such account has not been established.

## Liani Guardian

An emerald-furred wolf watches over the Liani forest realm. Its jade eyes are said to perceive deception, and its movements suggest close attunement to the surrounding life. The extent of its guardianship is unknown.

## Liani Predator

A powerful emerald-furred wolf hunts within the Liani woods. Its coloring conceals it in green shadow, while its patient movement and strong body make it an effective stalker. Guardianship should not be assumed merely from its resemblance to the Liani Guardian.
