# Unresolved Creature Traditions

These names remain undeveloped worldbuilding possibilities. They do not yet establish new species, societies, or relationships to the four peoples.

## Northern Names

Blazeclaw Beta, Greenmane Herbalist, Aurora Stalker, and Thunderpaw Shaman are preserved names without settled descriptions. It remains unclear whether they name individuals, kinds of creature, or titles.

## Peoples and Occupations

Glacier Shamans, Snowborn Hunters, and Frost-Touched Hermits remain unplaced names. Their ancestry, customs, and relationship to the Frostweavers are not established. Frostweaver Remnants is a collective description, not proof that all survivors share one condition.

## Names in Need of Reconciliation

Snowmaw Alpha, Glacierfang, Wintermane, Iceclaw Sovereign, and Winterworg were proposed alternatives for the [Frostfang Alpha](Everwinter_Beings.md#frostfang-alpha). They do not currently designate five additional animals. Older faun, dworv, fae, and giant terminology also needs reconciliation before it defines additional peoples.
