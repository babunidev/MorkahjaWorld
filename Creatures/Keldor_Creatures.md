# Keldor Creatures

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Unhinged Gobbl

A curse-distorted Gobbl is described with glowing green eyes, a hunched body, old scars, and restless clawed hands. This account concerns an afflicted individual, not the character of all Gobbls.

## Fearfeaster Gobbl

The Fearfeaster's elongated limbs, decaying-vegetation appearance, needle-like talons, and fixed grin distinguish it from ordinary Gobbl accounts. It is said to feed upon fear as well as life. The mechanism of that feeding remains uncertain.

## Firecrest Bloodbeak

A black-feathered rooster with sharp spurs serves as a bandits' lookout in [Keldor](../Places/Keldor.md). Village accounts describe a bargain of warnings in exchange for protection and food. Whether it communicates through ordinary calls or something more is unclear.

## Tempest Furypanther

An obsidian-coated panther carries storm-charged tentacles along its spine. Electrical energy gathers around its powerful body, and its roar resembles thunder. Keldor accounts also call it the Tempest Fury.

## Timberclaw Wretch

The Wretch has wooden limbs, a bark-like face, and glowing eyes set in knots. Sap-like fluid runs from its mouth, and splintered claws grasp at living flesh. Its form suggests forest life distorted by corruption, but its original nature is unknown.

## Auraglow Mireling

A small, Gobbl-like being absorbs energy from a shimmering pond in Keldor. Gold light spreads over its skin and eyes as it stands in the water. The resemblance does not establish that it is a Gobbl or explain the pond's power.

## Echowraith Skulker

A spectral-clawed predator moves through Keldor with abrupt, disjointed motions. Glowing eyes and guttural sounds betray its approach. Its origin and whether its body is material, spiritual, or both remain unknown.

## Lumbering Necrobrute

An immense decaying body resembling an Ogru moves without apparent awareness. Accounts attribute its movement to an unseen necromantic force. Its original identity is unknown; its appearance alone does not establish the fate of a particular Ogru.

## Blightwatcher Raven

Keldor's corruption has turned this raven toward predation upon living flesh. Its feathers appear to move independently, and blue light shines from its eyes. The distorted call and sickle-like talons distinguish it from ordinary forest birds.

## Roarback Mauler

A huge brown bear in southern Keldor bears an unnatural hump and greatly elongated claws. Its roar resembles a cry of pain. The account presents a creature afflicted by the forest's corruption rather than an ordinary bear lineage.

## Gloomweaver Colossus

A towering mass of sinewy tentacles is anchored among Keldor's blighted roots. Two lidless green eyes occupy an otherwise featureless head. Flesh and dark magic appear interwoven, but the creature's origin is unknown.
