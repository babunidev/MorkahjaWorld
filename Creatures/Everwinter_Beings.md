# Everwinter Beings

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Frostfang Alpha

An enormous, intelligent wolf-like predator hunts the southern reaches of [Everwinter Valley](../Places/Everwinter_Valley.md). Thick white fur conceals a powerful body, and sapphire eyes distinguish the Frostfang in accounts. Winterworg is another name attached to it; other proposed names have not been established as separate beasts.

## Misthowl

A translucent wolf moves through Everwinter's fog, almost indistinguishable from the mist. Her mournful howls are understood as warnings of storms and avalanches. Whether she causes these events or senses them remains unknown.

## Winterwisp Warden

A vortex of snow and frost with bright blue eyes guards the [Icebound Nymph Queen](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#icebound-nymph-queen). It moves silently across frozen ground and is closely bound to its mistress. The queen's realm and the origin of that bond are not yet described.

## Spirit Stag

A white stag with blue, frost-crystalled antlers is revered in Everwinter traditions. One account describes it wounded by a translucent arrow. Healing the stag is associated with divine favor, but the deity responsible is not identified.

## Frostclaw Behemoth

A colossal white bear of Everwinter, the Frostclaw has orange eyes and powerful claws reputed to tear steel. Its pale coat conceals it among snow and ice. It is one of the valley's most formidable described predators.
