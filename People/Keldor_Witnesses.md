# Keldor Witnesses

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## The Grumpy Fence

An elderly merchant in worn grey robes offers bones, skull talismans, and arcane curios on a moth-eaten cloth in [Keldor](../Places/Keldor.md). A battered wizard's hat conceals much of his face. The provenance and magical value of his wares are uncertain.

## Skyshard Guardian

An armored [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) woman resists Keldor's corruption with her remaining magic. A feathered crown distinguishes her appearance, though it does not establish royal status. Despite prolonged suffering, she greets others warmly and retains her resolve.

## Skyheart Drifter

An Aevis traveler maintains a wavering blue aura against the forest's influence. Finely woven garments move around her despite the stagnant air. Her kindness persists, but she needs help sustaining her protection.

## Meadowheart Lostling

A young [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) is described sitting in a Keldor glade, his songs reduced to quiet humming. The forest's magic has dimmed his vitality and hope. The title records his circumstances rather than a family name.

## Forestwander Sproutling

A Verdani child makes her way through Keldor with a knotted walking stick. Her tired humming preserves a trace of comfort while she seeks safety. Her origin and the events that brought her into the forest are unknown.

## Fringebound Escapee

A young Verdani woman escaped from Ogru captors and reached the edge of Keldor. Hunger and an injured ankle impede her journey. Her account preserves an individual's experience of captivity without defining all Ogrus by that act.

## Skylore Scholar

A young Aevis researcher studies Keldor's corrupted wildlife. Twilight coloring and a solitary vigil distinguish the account. Lost provisions and difficult conditions have left her vulnerable despite her scholarly determination.

## Verdantweave Envoy

The Envoy is an Aevis man working to restore corrupted plant life in Keldor. Scholarly insignia mark his robes. He needs protection while directing his magic into the damaged land; his title does not make him Verdani.

## Flameheart Purifier

A [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) woman uses controlled fire to burn dead, corrupted growth in Keldor. Leather armor and a deliberate manner reflect the hazards of her work. She seeks allies rather than assuming flame alone can make the forest safe.

## Cinderheart Vanguard

A young Rokur warrior continues to oppose Keldor's undead despite exhaustion. Worn armor and a diminished inner flame show the cost of her struggle. The title does not yet identify a wider military order.

## Shadowmarket Mystic

An unnamed woman trades powerful artifacts in Keldor's mists. Her clothes suggest long travel, and she appears unusually composed in the corrupted forest. The title does not establish a permanent settlement called Shadowmarket.
