# Everwinter Figures

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Everwinter Guardian

An ice-blue troll guards the entrance to [Everwinter Cave](../Places/Everwinter_Cave.md). Large ears and vigilant eyes help it notice approaching movement. Its breath produces little swirls of frost. White Ice Troll Guardian is another descriptive name for the same guardian in local accounts.

## Frostbite Fergus

Fergus is a friendly ice troll known for his appetite and hearty laugh. He is often described seated upon a pine log in [Everwinter Valley](../Places/Everwinter_Valley.md). Food and shared hospitality are central to accounts of his company.

## Kaldric of Nekalos

Once a Frostweaver mage, Kaldric now haunts Everwinter as a bitter, wailing spirit. Frosty winds coil around him, and his broken voice carries regret about the [Nekalos](../World_History/Frostweaver_Lore.md#nekalos) Mechanism. The catastrophe and his part in it remain unexplained.

## Frostlace

Frostlace is an alluring, dangerous figure of Everwinter with silver-white hair and luminous blue eyes. Promises of warmth and safety draw travelers farther into the cold. Her relationship to the Icebound Nymph Queen is not explicitly established.

## The Shattered Seer

The Seer of Snows once guided travelers through Everwinter. Dark magic has divided her consciousness into luminous fragments drifting around a shrine. Each holds traces of memory and the possibility of restoration; the cause of her shattering is unknown.

## Lyria of Nekalos

Lyria is an intangible figure whose steps leave snow undisturbed. Her pale form carries blue light, and mournful songs are heard around her appearances in blizzards. Her connection to Kaldric is remembered, but their relationship and fate remain uncertain.

## Jorvald

Jorvald is a Frostweaver scholar who guards sacred knowledge in Everwinter. Silver patterns cover his deep blue robes, and old scrolls and books accompany his work. He questions the intentions of visitors before sharing what he knows.

## Icebound Nymph Queen

The Queen is described as a beautiful predator who lures travelers to their deaths in the cold. The [Winterwisp Warden](../Creatures/Everwinter_Beings.md#winterwisp-warden) is named as her champion. Whether Frostlace is the same figure, a follower, or unrelated remains unresolved.
