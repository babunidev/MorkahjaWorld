## Nereval Elite

[Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) guardians wearing armor shaped from woodland materials watch over Nereval's sacred places. At the southeastern Vale sanctuary, a guardian recognizes the river rune as a sign of respect for the god. How this recognition relates to the sanctuary's healing rite is not fully established.
