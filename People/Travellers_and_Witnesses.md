# Travellers and Witnesses

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Flameveil Emberheart

Flameveil Emberheart is a [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) survivor of an attack on her village. Wounded while defending her home, she is remembered among scorched farmhouses in a torn blue dress. Her account centers on grief, resistance, and the need for recovery; the attackers and the village's name are not established.

## Stonefist Ironwill

Stonefist Ironwill is a [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) warrior associated with a ruined temple. His battered armor and injuries speak of prolonged conflict. He retains a determined bearing despite exhaustion, but his earlier life and the temple's identity remain unknown.

## The Elder Aevis of the Glade

An unnamed elder [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) rests in a quiet glade, humming melodies of her youth. Age has softened her once-bright coloring, while song and attentive hospitality retain their importance to her. Her history and home remain unrecorded.

## The Merchant at Celesterra

A venerable Aevis sells amulets and keepsakes at a market near the approaches to [Celesterra](../Places/Celesterra.md). His stories connect the objects to the city's traditions, although a practiced salesman's embellishment may accompany them. His name is unrecorded.

## Earthsinger

An earth-attuned pilgrim travels [Ethera's Vale](../Places/Etheras_Vale.md) in a weathered green cloak, carrying a resonant staff. He invites others to share artistry and expression. His age, people, and the origin of his gifts are unknown.

## Melody's Bloomwalker

A young [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) dancer travels the Vale in a deep-blue robe, with flowers woven into her hair and horns. Song, water, and spirit magic accompany her movement. She welcomes company through dance; Melody is not established as a separate person's name.

## Ember's Journeykindler

A Rokur pilgrim is remembered crossing the Vale while dangerously exhausted. Black smoke in her breath and a diminished inner fire reveal her condition. Faith sustains her determination, but her destination and chosen deity remain unknown.

## The Aevis at Zephyr's Embrace

An eastern Vale account describes an Aevis defending herself from bandits with gusts and air magic. [Zephyr's Embrace](../Magic/Aevis_Arts.md#zephyrs-embrace) is attached to the episode and to an airy protective practice; it does not establish a named settlement or the woman's identity.

## Joyful Riverkeeper

An aged Verdani storyteller rests beside a river in southeastern Ethera's Vale. He remembers lost loved ones with warmth and humor and welcomes passing company. The account emphasizes appetite, laughter, and continuity through shared memory; his personal name is unknown.
