# Elemental Pantheon

[The Elemental Pantheon](../Overview/World_Overview.md#the-elemental-pantheon) is a diverse and powerful collection of deities, each ruling over a specific aspect of the world. At its core, the pantheon is organized into four main tribunals, each anchored in one of the four material elements: fire, water, air, and earth. Aether is treated as quintessence, the connecting force through which the material elements become magic, inspiration, and higher transformation.

These tribunals work together to maintain the balance of Morkahja and guide the races of the world through their challenges and triumphs. Each race has a primary connection to one tribunal based on elemental origin, though individuals may pray to gods from other tribunals according to personal belief, craft, need, or circumstance.

When the gods of Morkahja visit the mortal world, they assume the forms of majestic dragons. Each dragon form reflects its deity's divine domain, allowing the god to interact with mortals in a powerful and awe-inspiring manner.

## Early Creation

[Ethera](../Places/Ethera.md) is among the gods' first living creations, formed from their shared intention to bring the elements into perfect balance. Her equal welcome draws the elemental streams toward her heart. The gods later found in the four peoples a motion still missing from this early creation. See the [creation overview](../Overview/World_Overview.md#creation-of-morkahja).

## The Ignis Council

**Tribunal of Fire, Light, and Chaos**

The Ignis Council embodies the raw power and energy of fire, the illuminating force of light, and the unpredictability of chaos. This tribunal is revered by the [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md), whose elemental origins are deeply intertwined with fire.

| Deity | Title | Domains | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fyraan | God of Fire | Passion, warmth, transformation, creativity, courage | Blazing flame |
| Solriel | Goddess of Light | Enlightenment, guidance, wisdom, hope, clarity | Radiant sun |
| Khaotys | God of Chaos | Unpredictability, change, freedom, creation, destruction | Swirling whirlwind of fire |

### Dragon Forms

**Fyraan** becomes a magnificent dragon with scales that shimmer like molten lava. Flames dance along its spine, and its breath is a torrent of fire.

**Solriel** takes the form of a graceful golden dragon with a radiant aura. Her scales are smooth and reflective, and her breath is a burst of blinding, purifying light.

**Khaotys** appears as a fierce, unpredictable dragon with ever-changing fiery colors across its scales. Its body seems to constantly shift, and its breath is a chaotic mix of fire and sparks capable of unexpected effects.

## The Abyssal Conclave

**Tribunal of Water, Growth, and Spirit**

The Abyssal Conclave represents the ever-flowing nature of water, the endless process of growth, and the mysteries of spirit. The [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md), with their connection to water, living ecosystems, and memory-bearing life, hold this tribunal in high regard.

It is also understood as the tribunal of living currents, governing adaptation, renewal, memory, vitality, and connection.

| Deity | Title | Domains | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nereval | God of Water | Fluidity, adaptation, healing, preservation, sustenance, protection | Flowing river |
| Sylvanus | God of Growth | Growth, renewal, vitality, natural cycles, becoming | Flourishing tree |
| Aerisara | Goddess of Spirit | Life force, consciousness, connection, inner renewal | Spiralling water drop |

### Dragon Forms

**Nereval** transforms into a sleek, serpentine dragon with deep blue scales resembling the depths of the ocean. Its body moves fluidly, its wings resemble flowing water, and its breath is a powerful torrent that can heal and protect.

**Sylvanus** transforms into a verdant dragon with scales resembling leaves, bark, and other elements of nature. Its body is covered in growing plants and moss, its wings seem made of intertwined branches, and its breath is a wave of rejuvenating energy that encourages growth and renewal.

**Aerisara** takes the form of a dragon with shimmering, iridescent scales. Its body is sleek and agile, its graceful wings seem to dance on the breeze, and its breath is a gentle mist that can heal and rejuvenate the spirit.

## The Celestial Convocation

**Tribunal of Air, Aether, and Order**

The Celestial Convocation symbolizes the freedom and vastness of air, the ethereal realm of aether, and the stability and harmony of order. The [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md), whose affinity lies with air, thought, and carefully shaped atmosphere, pay homage to this tribunal.

| Deity | Title | Domains | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aeriel | God of Air | Freedom, intellect, communication, change, wisdom | Soaring bird |
| Aetheria | Goddess of Aether | Cosmic energy, divine power, inspiration, celestial connection | Star-studded sky |
| Ordius | God of Order | Balance, discipline, harmony, structure, justice | Balanced scale |

### Dragon Forms

**Aeriel** becomes a majestic dragon with sky-blue scales and wings that stretch like a vast horizon. Its breath is a gust of wind that can carry messages, bestow knowledge, or sweep away obstacles.

**Aetheria** transforms into an awe-inspiring dragon with scales like the cosmos, filled with constellations and celestial patterns. Her wings seem to hold the fabric of the universe, and her breath is a surge of cosmic energy that can empower and inspire.

**Ordius** appears as a solid, steadfast dragon with metallic scales in shades of silver and gold. His form embodies balance and symmetry, and his breath is a beam of radiant energy that can impose order and restore harmony.

## The Terran Assembly

**Tribunal of Earth, Metal, and Darkness**

The Terran Assembly unites the gods of earth, metal, and darkness. They stand for the enduring stability of earth, the strength and versatility of metal, and the hidden depths of darkness. The [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md), with their ties to earth, metal, crystal, craft, buried memory, and hidden structure, worship the Terran Assembly and seek their guidance.

The Terran Assembly represents continuity through form: earth that endures, metal that adapts, and darkness that preserves what lies beneath sight.

| Deity | Title | Domains | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stonara | Goddess of Earth | Strength, stability, nurturing, foundation, protection | Mighty mountain |
| Ferrumis | God of Metal | Resilience, determination, innovation, craft, invention, mastery | Sturdy anvil |
| Umbrael | Goddess of Darkness | Mystery, secrets, hidden realms, intuition, truth beneath surfaces | Crescent moon |

### Dragon Forms

**Stonara** takes the form of a massive, powerful dragon with scales like slabs of stone. Her body is thick and muscular, her wingbeats can create small earthquakes, and her breath is a cloud of dust and gravel that can shield and fortify.

**Ferrumis** appears as a resilient dragon with scales made of metals ranging from iron and steel to precious ores. Its body is well-armored and strong, its wings resemble interlocking metal plates, and its breath is a shower of molten metal that can both create and destroy.

**Umbrael** appears as a mysterious, ethereal dragon with scales as black as the night sky. Its form is almost translucent, giving it an otherworldly appearance, and its breath is a cloud of inky darkness that can obscure vision and reveal hidden truths.

## Worship Across Morkahja

The [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) primarily honor the Ignis Council, looking to Fyraan for passion and transformation, Solriel for light and guidance, and Khaotys for the courage to embrace change.

The [Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) primarily honor the Abyssal Conclave, praying to Nereval for preservation and adaptation, Sylvanus for renewal and natural cycles, and Aerisara for spiritual connection.

The [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) primarily honor the Celestial Convocation, seeking Aeriel's guidance and freedom, Aetheria's divine inspiration, and Ordius's balance and order.

The [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) primarily honor the Terran Assembly, venerating Stonara for strength and support, Ferrumis for resilience and resourcefulness, and Umbrael for hidden knowledge, intuition, and what lies beneath the surface.

Worship in Morkahja is not rigidly confined by race. A Verdani healer might pray to Nereval for healing currents or Sylvanus for renewal, while a Rokur blacksmith might seek Ferrumis for inspiration in the forge. The worship of the gods is as diverse and complex as the peoples who inhabit the world.
