# Divine Orientation

Mortals sometimes read divine guidance in quiet signs within the world: a wind, the movement of grass, a rising bird, a brief reflection of light, or a pull sensed through stone. These interpretations belong to personal belief and elemental resonance, not to a demonstrated ability of an unmanifested deity to manipulate the physical world. Under the [Divine Laws](../Pantheon/Divine_Laws.md#the-nature-and-conduct-of-the-gods), a god must assume dragon form to act directly in Morkahja; otherwise the god may only give or take essence.

A traveler may see a white bird rise along a route, then notice reflected light picking out a passage farther on. A contrary wind may suggest a wrong turn. Elemental resonance can carry a sense of direction without spoken instruction.

Perceived guidance is distinct from remembered knowledge. A report preserves what someone knew at a particular time; a sign may be read in light of changing circumstances. Not every natural movement is a message, and the extent to which mortals recognize or understand such signs remains open.
