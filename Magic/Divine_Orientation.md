# Divine Orientation

The gods may guide mortals through quiet signs within the world: a wind, the movement of grass, a rising bird, a brief reflection of light, or a pull sensed through stone. Such guidance is intermittent, leaving silence and attention between signs.

A traveler may see a white bird rise along a route, then notice reflected light picking out a passage farther on. A contrary wind may suggest a wrong turn. Elemental resonance can carry a sense of direction without spoken instruction.

Divine guidance is distinct from remembered knowledge. A report preserves what someone knew at a particular time; a sign may respond to a changing present. The signs do not establish that every natural movement is a message, and the extent to which mortals recognize or understand this guidance remains open.
