# Common Arts

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Arcane Intellect

The absorption and mastery of strength, cunning or magic abilities, broadening expertise and enhancing diverse talents and capabilities.

## Haggling

The art of haggling with whimsical merchants, trading glittering treasure for mystical items and rare artifacts in the bustling markets of Morkahja.

## Find Treasure

Treasure seekers read disturbances in the land, explore concealed passages, and recognize signs of lost riches and forgotten relics.

## Open Lock

Lock opening relies on sensitive fingers, a knowledge of mechanisms, and careful manipulation of the parts concealed inside a lock.

## Healing Hands

Channels mystical energies to mend wounds and alleviate ailments, providing gentle restoration and rejuvenation through the touch of basic healing magic.

## Combat Training

Combat training develops bodily control, sound footing, and practiced offensive and defensive movements.

## Magic Missiles

Practitioners form and direct projectiles of arcane energy across a distance.

## Arcane Speechcraft

Proficient in the art of Speechcraft, the mage employs enchanted words and mystical charm spells to persuade and influence the thoughts and decisions of others.

## Arcane Healing

The mage harnesses potent arcane energies to perform advanced and powerful healing magic, mending wounds and ailments with remarkable skill and effectiveness.

## Dimension Door

The mage enables the creation of rifts in space to swiftly traverse obstacles and distances within the mystical realm.

## Charge

A sudden advance uses speed, weight, and commitment to carry a warrior into close combat.

## Improvised Weapons

Resourceful fighters turn ordinary objects into weapons through an understanding of weight, reach, and the materials around them.

## Battledance

The warrior performs an enchanting martial art dance, blending grace and power, captivating and inspiring those who witness it, boosting morale and energizing allies for battle.

## Defensive Stance

Adopting a solid, unyielding stance that enhances the warrior's defense, providing exceptional protection against enemy attacks and incoming threats during combat.

## Backstab

A concealed attacker strikes an unsuspecting opponent from behind, relying on surprise and precision.

## Cunning Lockpicking

Using the Rogue's finesse and expertise, they skillfully manipulate locks, unlocking doors and containers with precision, often without the need for conventional keys.

## Deception and Betrayal

Deception conceals intent through falsehood, misleading appearances, and the manipulation of trust.

## Acrobatic Climb

A remarkable climbing skill that combines agility, balance, and precision, enabling the Rogue to ascend challenging surfaces with grace and efficiency.

## Climb

Climbers develop grip, balance, and an understanding of surfaces to ascend difficult terrain.

## Helping Hand

Offers assistance to those in need, embodying compassion and cooperation, whether lending physical aid or moral support in times of challenge.

## Nurture

Cultivates the art of nurturing through culinary skills, creating nourishing meals and refreshing drinks that comfort, sustain, and rejuvenate.

## Purge Magic

Practitioners direct a cleansing force against spells and enchantments, disrupting the magical energies sustaining them.

## Hunt

Harnesses tracking abilities, stealth, and knowledge of prey to efficiently pursue, capture, or eliminate wildlife and creatures in a challenging, primal pursuit.

## Convincing Speech

The skill of persuasive and eloquent speech, capable of swaying hearts and minds with compelling words and convincing arguments.

## Stealth

Mastery of the art of stealth, allowing one to move silently and unseen, blending into shadows and avoiding detection with finesse and skill.

## Warmth

Utilizes techniques and knowledge to maintain comfort in cold environments, preventing frostbite and discomfort while ensuring the warmth of body and spirit.

## Inspiration

The power to inspire and uplift others, igniting their passion, courage, and creativity with words and actions, fostering motivation and unity among companions.

## Purifying Cleanse

Channels a purifying force to burn away impurities, curses, and harmful energies, cleansing objects or beings of corrupting influence.
