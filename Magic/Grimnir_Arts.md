# Grimnir Arts

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Mountain Strike

Summoning the unyielding might of [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) ancestors, this blow mimics the crushing weight of rocks falling from the sky.

## Ancestors' Muse

Grimnir draw on ancestral wisdom to awaken passion, confidence, and creative vision in those around them.

## Gem Seeker Instinct

Echoing the depths of Grimnir mines, this instinct magnifies the presence of hidden treasures or ancient passageways.

## Stone Whisperer's Touch

Listening to the silent murmurs of locks, Grimnir hands unlock secrets as if conversing with the mechanism itself.

## Orator of Bedrock

Words resonate like the deep rumblings of rocks, allowing Grimnir to influence and move hearts like shifting tectonic plates.

## Lustrous Gaze

Grimnir hunters focus their gem-bright gaze to follow prey and read its movement.

## Heartstone Purification

Grimnir channel the purity of crystal, negative energies dissipate, returning one to their natural balance.

## Cliffhanger's Grasp

Mimicking the steadfast grip of age-old roots in rocky crevices, Grimnir scale sheer faces with an innate stone-born mastery.

## Mineral Mend

Grimnir use suitable minerals and healing geomancy to mend fractures and restore coherence to an injured living stone body.

## Lavastone Culinary

Cooking with geothermal warmth and precious Skyland spices, Grimnir dishes invigorate, tasting like the very heart of the earth.

## Shadowstone Blend

Grimnir quiet their presence through their affinity with stone, blending with the stillness of the surrounding mineral landscape.

## Grimnir's Aid

Drawing upon the unity and kinship deeply embedded in Grimnir culture, one calls forth assistance, ensuring no ally stands alone in their time of need.

## Stoneborn Resilience

Grimnir reinforce and brace their mineral bodies against injury.

## Earthshaping

Suitable stone and mineral can be shaped into simple tools or weapons through geomantic craft.

## Stone Sense

Contact with material allows a Grimnir to sense movement and physical disturbances carried through it.

## Mineral Affinity

An understanding of natural materials allows useful stone and minerals to be recognized and assembled for survival.

## Inner Harmony

Prayer-like attunement and the support of companions restore the inner coherence that sustains individuality.

## Elemental Sustenance

Food, suitable minerals, and water are prepared together to nourish and strengthen another Grimnir.

## Echo Attunement

Grimnir share knowledge through resonant contact and the interpretation of memory.

## Resonant Touch

Touch carries rhythmic music and shared resonance, joining expression with companionship.

## Living Stone Adornment

Shaping, polishing, and setting mineral or crystal into the living body joins lasting refinement with personal expression.
