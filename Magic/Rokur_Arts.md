# Rokur Arts

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Lava Surge

Channels the fiery essence of the [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md), enhancing strikes with molten fury, scorching foes with each powerful blow.

## High Jump

Due to their immense strength, Rokur can perform incredible leaps to reach great heights or cover vast distances.

## Thunderous Roar

The Rokur shouts with the power of flames, intimidating everyone around.

## Luminous Gaze

The Rokur draws from Solriel's radiant light, illuminating hidden paths, secrets, or treasures, even in the darkest of places.

## Fiery Pursuit

Utilizing the fiery nature of the Rokur to chase prey in their tracks, ensuring a swift and precise hunt.

## Embercleanse

The Rokur harnesses fire's purifying properties, burning away impurities, toxins, and harmful energies from objects or beings.

## Melt Lock

The Rokur uses the fire's heat to melt the lock to open it.

## Inferno Eloquence

Rokur wield the persuasive power of flames, captivating and swaying audiences with a fiery passion and intensity in their words.

## Hearth's Embrace

Rokur channel the warmth and nurturing nature of fire, mending wounds and providing comfort to the injured.

## Caldera Culinary

Rokur utilize volcanic heat for cooking, imbuing dishes with a unique blend of smoky flavors and elemental energy.

## Aurora's Whisper

Calls upon the mesmerizing dance of northern lights, igniting creativity and sparking novel ideas in the Rokur and their allies.

## Ember Aid

Calls upon the unity and camaraderie inherent to Rokur culture, bolstering allies with warmth, strength, and protection in their time of need.
