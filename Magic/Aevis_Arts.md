# Aevis Arts

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Cyclone Strike

[Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) channel wind into a concentrated blow, surrounding the impact with a staggering rush of air.

## Skybound Ascension

Leveraging their connection with the element of air, Aevis can make even the steepest surfaces feel like gentle slopes, allowing them to climb with unparalleled grace and ease.

## Voice of the Breeze

Aevis words carry the mesmerizing cadence of the air, making their arguments, negotiations, and tales exceptionally persuasive and enchanting.

## Echoes of the Azure

By recounting tales of the air or performing their Airdance, Aevis can inspire those around them, boosting morale, creativity, and determination.

## Skyward Mending

Drawing from the purity of the air, Aevis can channel a soothing wind that mends wounds and rejuvenates the weary.

## Celestial Brew

Using their affinity with the air, Aevis can masterfully blend ingredients, enhancing flavors to create dishes that taste otherworldly and can even provide minor magical effects.

## Gale Seeker

With keen senses honed by the air's embrace, Aevis can track prey across great distances, the winds carrying scents and sounds directly to them.

## Wind's Cleansing

Aevis summons a gust of pure, untainted air that dispels toxins, curses, and corruptions, restoring purity to the affected area.

## Breath of the Horizon

Aevis can read the memories of the air, revealing hidden paths, objects, or traces of magic otherwise unseen to the naked eye.

## Aetherial Unlocking

By aligning with the air's subtle vibrations, Aevis can unlock or seal doors, chests, and gates without a physical key.

## Whispered Veil

Aevis blend with the ambient breezes, their presence becoming as subtle and intangible as a fleeting gust, making them nearly undetectable to foes.

## Zephyr's Embrace

Aevis can summon gentle winds to aid those in need, whether by cushioning a fall, guiding lost travelers, or amplifying another's abilities temporarily.
