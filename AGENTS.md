# MorkahjaWorld — AI collaboration rules

**Human creative:** Babuni  
**Repository purpose:** Original fantasy world-building archive

## Scope and authorship

The human creative determines Morkahja's world, characters, places, history, unresolved questions, and creative direction. AI may help research, organize, document, edit, and maintain material when directed; its contribution must not be mistaken for an independently approved creative decision.

The repository is **not a placeholder**. It contains an existing lore collection imported on 1 November 2026. Do not replace that material or assume a playable game exists.

## Working boundaries

- **Do not rewrite lore** during project-documentation maintenance. Limit edits to project documents unless a separate instruction explicitly authorizes lore changes.
- Treat established descriptions, working possibilities, and deliberately unresolved mysteries differently. Do not invent missing canon.
- Read the relevant source files before describing Morkahja's world. Prefer direct links to existing indexes and entries.
- Distinguish MorkahjaWorld from other repositories named Morkahja or Ethera. Within this archive, Ethera is also an in-world figure; repository names alone do not establish a content transfer.
- Record the source and authorship of future imported work. Git history from an upload is not proof of who originally drafted each passage.
- Do not claim builds, tests, implementation, rights clearance, or publishing checks that have not been performed.

## Project documentation and journal

Use `README.md`, `CONCEPT.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and `docs/` for project status and development process. Preserve important historical decisions even when later developments make earlier status descriptions obsolete.

Write public-facing development narratives in the **human creative's first-person perspective**, with natural sentences that **never begin with “I” or “My.”** Describe the creative's choice, the AI's actual assistance, implementation, and verification without attributing the AI's actions to the human. Refer to the person making the work as a **creative**, not a “creator.”

Technical instructions may use a neutral voice. Keep the journal concise, publication-safe, and faithful to what can be verified.
