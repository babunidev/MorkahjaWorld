# Frostweaver Lore

The Frostweavers are an ancient presence tied to [Everwinter Valley](../Places/Everwinter_Valley.md), its ruins, and its dangerous relics. Their exact nature remains open: they may have been a people, order, civilization, magical lineage, or ruling class of the [Frostwind Mountains](../Places/Frostwind_Mountains.md).

## Nekalos

Nekalos is an ancient Frostweaver figure or lineage associated with several major relics and spirits:

- Nekalos Mechanism
- [Seal of Nekalos](../Objects/Frostweaver_Relics.md#seal-of-nekalos)
- [Kaldric of Nekalos](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#kaldric-of-nekalos)
- [Lyria of Nekalos](../People/Everwinter_Figures.md#lyria-of-nekalos)

## Known Relics and Concepts

- Nekalos Mechanism - remembered by Kaldric as a mistake.
- Seal of Nekalos - ancient knowledge unlocked through the Sigil Lens of Nekalos.
- [Frostweaver Palace Ruins](../Places/Frostweaver_Palace_Ruins.md) - monumental ruins with inscription tables.

## Open Questions

- Was Nekalos a person, dynasty, school, or machine lineage?
- What did the Nekalos Mechanism do, and why was it a mistake?
- Are Kaldric and Lyria victims, guardians, architects, or warnings?
