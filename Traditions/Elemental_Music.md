# Elemental Music

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Harmonic Concerts

Musicians gather in [Aetherion](../Places/Aetherion.md)'s natural amphitheater with instruments attuned to elemental forces. Their harmonies can influence air and weather as well as emotion. Traditions attribute the averting of foretold disasters to such performances, though no universal power over prophecy is established.
