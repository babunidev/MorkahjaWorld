# Healing and Hospitality

[All lore](../Lore_Index.md)

## Rokur Exhaustion

Excessive exertion of fire magic can leave a [Rokur](../Races/Rokur.md) scorched and dangerously depleted. Accounts describe dimmed flame, blackened surfaces, lingering smoke, and strength consumed by the very heat that sustains the body. Rest, nourishment, salves, and careful healing can be necessary even for accomplished fire-workers.

## Aevis Depletion

An [Aevis](../Races/Aevis.md) who spends their magical strength may become breathless, dim, and physically vulnerable. Gentle care and restorative treatment help stabilize a person whose usual lightness and luminous presence have faded.

## Grimnir Injury

A mineral body can be exhausted or injured despite its strength. A wounded [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) may need support, material repair, and rest rather than further effort. Accounts of battered armor and a failing stance emphasize that endurance still has limits.

## Verdani Illness

[Verdani](../Races/Verdani.md) illness may show as pallor, dulled hair, weak movement, and a faltering connection to surrounding growth. Nearby plants are sometimes described as wilting with the sufferer. Healing, restorative preparations, and companionship belong together in their care.

## Verdani Exhaustion

A Verdani weakened at the edge of [Evergrove](../Places/Evergrove.md) is described with faded jade coloring and shallow breath. Proximity to living growth alone does not ensure recovery. Direct care and nourishment can be needed to restore the person's connection and strength.

## After Battle

The dimming of an Aevis aura can reveal severe fatigue after conflict. Healing, rest, reassurance, and practical assistance are all part of care. A person's usual grace should not be mistaken for immunity to injury.

## Recovery from Corrupted Violence

An account of a Grimnir injured by a Crumblegheist describes a shattered cave and a failing mineral body. Companionship and material healing are needed after such violence. The sufferer's identity and the date of the clash are unrecorded.

## Care after Raids

An elderly Grimnir defender is remembered sitting at the entrance to an underground dwelling after raiders took his belongings. Hunger and loss weakened him as much as age. The account places food, assistance, and dignity at the center of communal recovery.

## Lost Children in Karrhu

Accounts of travel through [Karrhu](../Places/Karrhu.md) include an abandoned Verdani child struggling with hunger in the marsh. Nourishment and safe guidance matter as much as magical healing. The story does not establish a named settlement or a separate kind of Verdani.

## The Tired Forge

A Rokur smith's diminished flame can reveal exhaustion before the hammer falls silent. A meal, rest, and companionship restore more than physical strength. Forge-side stories include affectionate jokes about Fyraan as well as formal reverence.

## Crossing Ohgar

Even a Grimnir may be dangerously depleted by [Ohgar](../Places/Ohgar.md)'s heat, abrasive winds, and lack of water. Accounts of a young traveler in distress emphasize the importance of water and guidance through the desert. Mineral strength does not remove the need for elemental balance.

## Valor and Grief

Rokur accounts describe a battle-weary warrior whose resolve returns through remembered sagas and martial dance. Such performances connect individual suffering to the community's history. They offer encouragement without erasing the cause of grief.

## Lost Souls of Everwinter

This name is used for living travelers overcome by fatigue, cold, and despair in a mountain pass. Their situation is not evidence that they are ghosts. Stories of courage and practical guidance can help them continue toward shelter.
