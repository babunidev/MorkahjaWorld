# Grimnir Life and Care

[Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) bodies are living arrangements of stone, metal, and crystal. Food sustains energy and enjoyment; minerals replenish bodily substance; water carries dissolved nutrients through fine internal channels; geomancy maintains the coherence of the whole.

## Nourishment

Meals combine roots, grain, mushrooms, fruit, and other produce with carefully prepared mineral ingredients. Grinding, warmth, water, and geomantic activity work together within the digestive chamber. Minerals absorbed from the environment also help maintain the body. Children and injured adults benefit from materials suited to growth or repair. Preparing minerals so that they nourish and taste good is a culinary craft.

## Resting Stones and Resonance Baths

Sleep quiets awareness while gentle vibrations redistribute minerals, mend small fractures, and restore coordination. A low hum may accompany this work. Shaped resting stones carry soothing vibrations through mineral layers fitted to the body. A mineral-rich resonance bath provides deeper care after exhaustion or injury. A companion may place a hand against the resting person and match their quiet rhythm.

## The Stonebound Condition

At the surface, stone meets wind, rain, warmth, and growing things. Prolonged elemental isolation deep underground can allow earth to dominate: movement slows, the body becomes bulkier and heavier, and awareness turns inward toward primal stone. Ordinary cave visits and overnight shelter do not cause this transformation. If individuality remains and the person wishes to return, patient care and renewed elemental balance can restore awareness and movement. Long isolation requires long recovery.

## Inner Harmony and Adornment

[Inner Harmony](../Magic/Grimnir_Arts.md#inner-harmony) is a prayer-like restoration of the coherence, motivation, and individuality that animate the body. Companionship and communal life support it. [Living Stone Adornment](../Magic/Grimnir_Arts.md#living-stone-adornment) shapes, sets, polishes, and refines the living mineral body; its lasting changes join personal expression with practical bodily care.
