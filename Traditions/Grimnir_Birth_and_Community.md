# Grimnir Birth and Community

Two willing [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) contribute fragments of their geomantic identity to a third life that forms outside their bodies. The emerging individual inherits from both while developing an identity of their own.

## The Growing Life

A mineral-rich resonance bath provides the material setting. Stone supplies substance, water carries dissolved minerals, warmth supports transformation, and air and surface exposure maintain balance. A small crystalline growth gathers material as awareness develops. Its earliest communication may be a faint vibration, a response to touch, or an answering rhythm.

The creators prepare the nesting place, gather food and minerals, seek useful knowledge, and perform nourishing resonance together. Prepared stone and resonant crystals sustain the child while its caregivers travel. Other members of the community share in this care.

## Birth and Upbringing

Several members of the community participate in the final shaping. The child joins the settlement as a child of the community, while the two creators remain the sources of its inherited identity. Independent form and awareness distinguish the newborn from the growing presence in the bath.

Children develop through helping, learning, exploring, and forming relationships. Their abilities become apparent through participation in village life. A coming-of-age rite recognizes adulthood and the emerging pattern of a person's capabilities; the precise form of the rite remains to be developed.
