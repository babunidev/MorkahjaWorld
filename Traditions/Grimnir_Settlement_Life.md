# Grimnir Settlement Life

Early [Grimnir](../Races/Grimnir.md) communities lived at the surface, before the development of the Skylands. Accessible stone and mineral-rich ground lay within wind, warmth, moisture, and growing life. Their settlements grew through shaping the matter around them.

## Shelters and Shared Spaces

Roofs, shallow carved recesses, and shaped supports protected inhabitants while retaining contact with the surrounding elements. Layered rock carried weight, worked joins connected components, and crystal supported resonance. Walls and openings kept visible traces of shaping, repair, and individual workmanship.

A home could also be a workshop. A gathering place could host a shared meal, knowledge exchange, or celebration. Prepared resting surfaces, mineral-rich nursery baths, stone cooking vessels, and accessible memory stones met bodily and communal needs. Buildings grew through components that people shaped, carried, and installed.

## Sound, Gesture, and Care

Tool rhythms, mineral scraping, low humming, and stone contact made work audible. Touch and vibration offered reassurance and shared timing. Stories carried through memory stones gave communal knowledge an audible presence. The Spiral Forge-Ring, luminous seams, and deliberately shaped surfaces expressed cultural identity.

Contribution included craft, food preparation, tending children, keeping knowledge, company, and patient attention. Recognition and trust grew through shared experience. Names and personal histories became known through exchange rather than through appearance alone.

## Continuity with Later Cities

Later Skylands extended this material culture into sky-core anchors, crystal lattices, bridges, and districts held in deliberate balance. [Aetherion](../Places/Aetherion.md)'s [Stonebound Terraces](../Places/Aetherion.md#stonebound-terraces) expressed related traditions through surface foundations, halls, and archives. These later achievements should not be assumed to have existed in the earliest grounded settlements.
